## Fixes and improvements

- The Water Lure Torch checks ranks like natural spawning: it does not summon a mob if a player who has not yet reached it in the story is nearby.
- Dream Water: small mobs stay at the surface at a third of their height and no longer suffocate, from the depth they are pushed out to the surface. Dropped items and experience go up, toward the lake bottom.
- The Ancient Dramcryx has a boss-style health bar like Divine bosses.
- Near overworld lairs the game no longer places abandoned mineshafts and dungeons with a spawner and a chest. Affects new chunks.
- Arcanite Pickaxe: new texture; breaks all unbreakable blocks of Arcana dungeons (except the portal), and they drop whole.
- Wanderer summon egg: in the home biome of a Twilight dimension the Wanderer of that world appears with trades, elsewhere - a Wanderer in a green robe without trading.
