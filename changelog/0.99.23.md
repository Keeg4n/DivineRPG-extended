## Big batch of fixes and suggestions

### New
- Water Lure Torch: a water ingot above a water ifrit rod. Right-click with Arcanium - burns for a minute (can be topped up to 10 minutes); while it burns, Divine mobs of this biome spawn more often within 24 blocks; bubbles instead of smoke.
- Frost Torch and Dream Torch (4 each) with animated textures, repel Divine monsters in their own world.
- Arcana fountain halls: occasionally a regular room becomes a hall with a bowl of Arcane Concentrate. In the concentrate arcana regenerates quickly, Arcana fish are caught only in it and only with an Arcana Fishing Rod. Affects new chunks, frequency is `arcanaFountainChance`.
- Arcana dungeon spawners are broken with the Arcanite Pickaxe. It is sold by the War General for Arcanium to those who defeated all Mortum bosses.
- Map to the Jungle Temple (the Ancient Dramcryx lair): paper surrounded by cocoa beans.
- Suspicious Sandstone: 5 smooth sandstone in a U shape = 5 pieces.

### Changes
- In Divine worlds (Iceika, Twilight dimensions, Arcana, Vethea) only Divine and add-on mobs spawn on their own, mobs from other mods stay out. The whitelist is `divineSpawnWhitelist`.
- Halite Multitool: efficiency 60, right-click tills soil like a hoe.
- Dream Carrots and the Dream Melon Stem behave like vanilla crops: they break if the farmland dries out or is trampled.
- Water in Eden is golden (its own animated texture).
- Apalachia Cart: middle click in creative gives its item, a mob on a wall and ceiling does not fall inside; with a player it starts moving only on the "forward" button in the direction of the view.
- Dream Water: in third and first person the camera is at the head, the flipped model does not turn the head mirrored, the line comes from the hand; mobs in it are flipped too. The Dream Water block is visible in NEI.
- The Wanderer has 1000 health (including those already placed).
- In Apalachia the sanctum seal is also in every ruin chest.
- Completed the Russian translation of everything Divine had without it; names are unified: Vethea, Skythern, Vamacheron, Soul Fiend, Nether Fire. The add-on's English translation is complete.

### Fixes
- Lethe deals damage in any world, only Mortum mobs count as villagers.
- After generation the Frozen Throne restores walls and vault from the blueprint and removes flowed-in lava and water. Affects new chunks.
- The Wind Lord on death no longer plays the dragon sound to the whole server - only those who hit him hear it.
- Tunnels to the Watcher's observatory and the Fire Chamber cut through the hall wall all the way.
- Regular water no longer clings to a Dream Water source.
