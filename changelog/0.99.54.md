## Corrupted gear - gear of darkness

Corrupted shards drop from the Grue, which lives in the pitch darkness of caves - and items made from them are now strong in darkness.

- The Corrupted Pickaxe, Axe and Shovel dig faster the darker it is where you stand: in full darkness 2.5 times, up to light 12 - as usual. In bright light (13 and above) they dull: x0.8.
- Eyes of the Grue: dig in darkness (light 4 and below) with a Corrupted tool - night vision as long as you keep digging. With the Death Sword or the Corrupted Maul - the same when you strike in darkness.
- All of this is written in the tooltips.
