## Boss arena

- Applies to all Divine and add-on bosses, except the Hive Queen (she has her own fight reset).
- The arena is the lair hall; for the Frozen Throne - the dome up to the ring of pylons; for the others - a circle of 24 blocks around the boss spawn point.
- You cannot leave the arena of a living boss: at the edge a fighter is pushed back toward the center with a message. You can only leave by teleport, by death or by quitting the game.
- When no fighters are left on the arena, a warning arrives, and after 60 seconds the boss disappears without trophies and without a counted victory. If even one returns, the countdown starts over.
- Summoning outside a lair requires a flat free space within 8 blocks; in a lair it is refused only if the spawn point is built over. On refusal the summon item is not consumed.
- Settings in the bosses section: `bossArenaLeaveSeconds` (0 - immediately, -1 - never), `bossArenaHold`, `bossArenaRadius`, `bossSummonClearRadius`.
