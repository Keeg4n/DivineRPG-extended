## Corrupt altar: higher prices

- The most expensive item enchantment costs 120 levels instead of 50, and a book 150 instead of 120. Slot caps: item 40 / 80 / 120, book 70 / 110 / 150. A book still starts at 30.
- The strength of the enchantments did not change: only the price went up.
- Experience is still deducted "from zero": an enchantment for 120 levels costs 34440 experience, for 150 levels 58245. At a high level this is fewer levels than the price: from level 150, an item enchantment for 120 leaves you at about level 103.
- Old values in the settings file are replaced by the new ones automatically. If you set your own prices, they stay.
