## Arcana swords: arcana in melee

- Full Korma set and the Arcana Flow perk (Wanderer armor): Arcana sword hits no longer stop arcana regeneration. Before, every expense (a hit with the saber, blade, shadow sword, the arcane strike of the Storm Sword and the Livicia Sword) triggered a 2 second pause, and in melee the bar only drained: the most expensive swords (12 arcana per hit, two hits per second) emptied it in about eight seconds. Now with Korma (expenses 20% cheaper) and with Arcana Flow (regeneration twice as fast) any Arcana sword at the usual hit rhythm strikes without stopping. Shooting, staffs and Storm Sword lightning trigger the pause as before; a sword hit neither resets nor extends someone else's pause.
- The tooltip of the Korma set pieces says what the full set gives.
- Hotfix 0.99.104: against Arcana bosses the sword's arcane strike was assigned on every click, so by spam-clicking it hit many times per second, and the shadow sword did not spend arcana on them. Now the hits follow the usual rhythm (after a hit the boss is invulnerable for half a second, two clicks per tick are one hit), and the shadow sword pays its 12 arcana.
