## Arcana spawners can be moved, the Arcanite Pickaxe only destroys

- Arcana spawners are moved with the "Relocation" focus from Thaumic Tinkerer and the Blood Magic teleposer, including where the teleposer forbids unbreakable blocks (RedServer).
- As before, only the Arcanite Pickaxe breaks them. The Blood Magic bound pickaxe, the Thaumcraft drill and other miners do not take them.
- Arcana blocks and spawners broken with the Arcanite Pickaxe no longer drop, with Silk Touch too. It mines regular blocks like all pickaxes.
- The focus does not take other Arcana blocks.
