## Changed

- Cannons fire like blitzes: every shot is a fan of 3 pellets (blitzes have 5). Affected: Ice, Frostclaw, Fractite, Ghast, Crabclaw and Bowhead cannons. Like blitz pellets, they fly about 10 blocks, all of them can hit one target, they do not knock back, and the side pellets deal less to bosses. One shot still costs one round of ammo.
- Not affected: staffs, Maelstrom, Golden Fury, the Corrupted Cannon (it already fires several bullets) and Vethea weapons.

## Other

- Selftests for guns, blitz pellets and loaded ammo were run.
