## Ayeraco horde, End armor and a batch of fixes

### Ayeraco horde and End armor
- The Ayeraco Aerie is a distant island 1000 blocks from the center of the End, reached by a chain of 5 respite islets: you can fly there in angelic armor. Around the island are five colored pillars. Affects new chunks.
- The horde circles above the island and attacks in turn; it teleports only above the island and does not go into the void.
- Abilities by color: green heals the pack, blue deflects projectiles, red hits x1.5 in rage, yellow dives and slows, purple pulls a sibling over to you. While a color's pillar is intact, its Ayeraco regenerates. The last survivor takes the strength of the fallen.
- End armor of its own color: hits on an Ayeraco of the same color +50% and protection from its ability; gray - +10% against any Ayeraco.
- Full End armor set (colors can be mixed): with an Ender sword in hand - a void step of 9 blocks for 25 arcana; fall into the abyss - the armor saves you onto solid ground.
- The Wraith Bone summons a wraith: it flies after its owner through walls and hits its target with arcane damage. Up to 2 wraiths (3 in the Korma set), each spends 3 arcana per second.

### Descriptions and potions
- Descriptions of Divine and add-on items are hidden under Shift, with the line "Shift - description" at the bottom. Numbers, enchantments, attributes and "From Vethea" are visible right away.
- Arcane potion effects became real Minecraft effects: visible in the inventory with their own icons, removed by milk. Effect IDs can be set in the config (`arcanePotionIds`). The icons by the arcana bar were removed.
- The potion name is Potion "Stone Skin", with strength and duration under it, like vanilla ones. Identical arcane potions stack up to 16.
- The Way Home, Call to Arms and Stillness potions were removed; already brewed ones remain, marked "This potion has been removed".
- New textures of Soul Silk and Sky String - a tangle of threads on a spool.

### Fixes
- The dummy is drawn like a live player: 64x64 skins, HD and Alex's slim arms. It counts damage from explosions and projectiles without an attacker; fire, lava and fall damage do not count.
- A hanging rail without neighbors turns to a new neighbor directly, without a turn; the turn holds if neighbors are broken.
- Lair maps skip locations in already generated chunks where a lair will not appear. The tomb under the pyramid loads missing chunks when a player is nearby.
