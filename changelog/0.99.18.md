## World torch particles

- World torches have their own particles instead of the regular flame: Eden's shoot rays from the sun, Wildwood's drip drops and float up moon sparks, Apalachia's is drawn into a rift, Skythern's has the wind blowing away little clouds, Mortum's drips blood and raises dark smoke, and the Nether Torch throws embers.
- Dream Water: the screen and controls no longer flip, the camera of the flipped player is in the head rather than in the feet. The player is still seen upside down.
