## Cave Angler

- A new bait "Cyclops Eye Bait": a shapeless craft, 1 cyclops eye shard - 2 baits. Strength 20.
- In caves (below height 48, no sky overhead) fish bite faster with the Cyclops Eye Bait: wait x0.6.
- There, a new fish is caught on this bait - the Cave Angler (12% of bites, grows with rare-catch fishing rods). Only in caves and only on the Cyclops Eye Bait. Eating it gives Night Vision for 3 minutes. A fish for Leorna's quests.
- NEI: the angler in the cave catch.
- Hotfix: released without a full run of the selftests. The new angler check and the fishing checks affected by the change passed; not checked in game.
