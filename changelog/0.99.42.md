## Easter eggs

- Realmite armor named `H.E.V` is the suit from Half-Life: its own model, the suit voice (system login, morphine at low health, fractures, critical health).
- A Realmite sword named `crowbar` is a crowbar.
- Meriks Missile named `rpg` is an RPG-7, the projectile flies as a PG-7 grenade.
- A Divan RPG on a sofa in a special house of the jungle underground city, which you can sit on.
- An Ender Bow arrow pins Ender mobs in place for 3 seconds, like a hit from an Ender sword.
