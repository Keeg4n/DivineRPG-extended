## Easter egg

- Musou no Hitotachi: the guard was redone after the in-game model (points taken from the picture):
  - instead of a single curl-spiral, three glowing purple tomoe commas with round heads: the upper one as an arch along the top edge, the right one with its tail toward the bronze bar, the lower one, the largest, as a hook under the neck;
  - the commas lie on a dark flat plate-neck between the handle and the blade (noticeably above the handle, narrowing toward the blade), with two spikes on top bent toward the handle and a spike below;
  - the bronze crossguard is a thin bar set diagonally across the end of the handle: the short end toward the handle on the spine side, the long end toward the blade on the blade side.
