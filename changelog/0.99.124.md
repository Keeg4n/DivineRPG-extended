## Miner fishing

Underground fishing now replaces the mine: the bobber height decides which ore is caught, and ore comes as blocks (smelting and doubling by machines work as with mined ore).

- Caves (water below height 48 with no sky overhead) are divided into tiers by bobber height: upper - 33-47, middle - 17-32, depths - 16 and below. The depths in full need a fishing strength of 40 and more; with less, the catch there is as in the middle caves.
- The Caves Crate became the Miner's Crate of its tier: 5-8 stacks of ore, and in 1 crate out of 8 also diamond, emerald or rupee ore; plus cavefish and sometimes Arcana bait, as in other crates.
  - Upper: coal, iron, amber and Thaumcraft aspect stones, a little redstone.
  - Middle: iron, coal, gold, lapis, redstone, realmite, Thaumcraft cinnabar, aspect stones.
  - Depths: redstone, gold, realmite, lapis, arlemite, rupee, diamond, emerald.
- The uncommon catch in caves is 1-2 ores of its tier (before, coal, redstone dust and an iron ingot). The rare and very rare catches are as before (ingots, diamond, emerald), and so is the Cave Angler on the Cyclops Eye Bait.
- The Lava Crate (Lava and Nether) is a miner's crate too: 5-8 stacks of quartz, nether and bloodstone ore, and in 1 out of 8 a very rare Nether catch.
- Ores of other mods are taken from the ore dictionary by themselves: in RedServer Magic these are Thaumcraft and Tinkers copper, tin and aluminum (with a small weight); silver and lead if they appear in the modpack.
- The three miner's crates have their own names, icons (the lid shows the tier ore: coal and iron, gold and lapis, diamond and redstone dust) and a tooltip with the tier height. Old cave crates open as the Miner's Crate of the middle caves.
- NEI shows crates by tier and the ore in the cave catch; in fishing debug mode the tier and bobber height are visible.
- Pace: with good tackle a bite every 10-15 s, a crate about every 10th - that comes to 4-6 ores per minute, like a leisurely mine. Bait is consumed on every bite.
