## Jack's scythe, Jack's sets, Satiety

- Jack O Man's scythe: a hit frightens with a 20% chance (with the full Jack O Man set - 50%). A frightened mob runs away from the attacker for 3 seconds (slightly faster than usual, with a wisp of smoke overhead), hits no one and takes no target; a creeper puts out its fuse. Then fear does not affect it for 5 seconds - otherwise an endless scythe would keep a crowd in fear forever. Bosses and mini-bosses are not frightened, and neither are players.
- The scythe with the full skeleton set: with a 25% chance a hit provokes - hostile mobs within 8 blocks attack whoever the scythe hit for 5 seconds (like mobs that a skeleton accidentally hit). Zombies and others whose vanilla attack only targets players and villagers attack too: if the mob has not hit by itself within a second, the hit is dealt for it with its own damage.
- The scythe with the full Withered Reaper set: with a 25% chance the mob fights for you for 3 seconds - it hits the nearest hostile mobs, and does not touch players, peaceful creatures and bosses (its damage to them is cancelled, and it does not target them). Those killed by it count as killed by you: experience and loot. A subdued creeper does not explode. After being subdued, the mob cannot be subdued again for 10 seconds.
- The scythe tooltip shows fear and the set properties; the property of the worn set is highlighted in green.
- Jack's sets got protection: in Divine all three had 0. Skeleman (from the start) - 35%, Jack O Man (after Carcinus) - 50%, Withered Reaper (after the Lich King) - 60%. For comparison: realmite 50%, Kraken 45%, the Frozen set and the jungle set 65%. The values are in the settings (skelemanArmor, jackOManArmor, witherReaperArmor).
- The Satiety perk (Wanderer armor, donor - the skeleton set) works in a new way: hunger is spent twice as slowly, you do not die from hunger or lose health, and you can sprint even with an empty hunger bar. Before, the perk slowly satisfied hunger by itself.
