## Training Dummy

- New item - the Training Dummy. Right-click a block to place your own copy: skin, worn armor, accessories, potion effects and arcana.
- A hit on the dummy is counted as if you were being hit: above its head are the damage of the last hit and damage per second.
- Right-click - reset the count, sneak + right-click - copy yourself again.
- No profit and no dupes: nothing can be taken off the dummy, it does not die and drops nothing, vampirism does not heal from it, thorns do not hurt the attacker.
- Only the one who placed the dummy can remove it: sneak and hit.
