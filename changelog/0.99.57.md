## Easter egg

- The RPG-7 was redrawn to match the real one: a black flared rear, a wooden overlay with three metal rings, a black tube with a coupling, two sights on top, a pistol grip with a trigger guard and a front wooden grip, in front an olive PG-7 grenade (a tail with fins, a bulb, a cone and a nose). Before it was a set of rectangular bars where the grenade, flare and tube were the same color.
- On the Divan RPG the RPG lies diagonally on its side, grips toward the edge of the seat, the grenade on the armrest.
- Meriks Missile renamed to rpg, in hand and on the ground, is now a volumetric RPG-7 of the same model (before - a flat icon), and the inventory icon was redrawn from it.
- The PG-7 grenade in flight has the same shape, with a tail tube and a cross of stabilizers.
