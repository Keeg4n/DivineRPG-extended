## Doom altar - after the Watcher

- The center of the doom altar recipe is now the Eye of the Watcher instead of dark stone (3 corrupted stones are still on top, the rest is dark stone). Before, the altar could be assembled right after the Lich King; now it comes after the Watcher: its 56 levels are just enough for an enchantment costing 50. Altars that are already placed are untouched. With corruptAltar=false the recipe stays as in Divine.
