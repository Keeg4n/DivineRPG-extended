## Changed
- Personal passes to the twilight worlds and Vethea, as the Arcana portal already had: a portal that someone else lit lets in only those who reached the world themselves. Eden - after the Watcher, Wildwood - after the Great Eden Cadillion, Apalachia - after the Wildwood Ancient Golem, Skythern - after the Apalachia Warrior King, Mortum - after the Skythern Wind Lord. A player who has not reached the world is not transferred by the portal and is told whom to defeat; leaving a world through its own portal always works. Before, on a server a newcomer walked through other players' portals into any world and took its chests, fish and ore.
- The Nightmare Bed lets into Vethea only those who defeated the bosses of the Spirit of the Universe themselves: the Ancient Entity, the Ancient Dramcryx, the King of Scorchers, Dramix, Parasecta and any three Mortum bosses. The refusal lists who is missing.
- A player who got into such a world in another way (a teleport, a command) is returned to the place where he last stood, or to his bed in the Overworld.
- A victory counts for everyone who hit the boss, so a group goes on together. Creative mode is not affected.
- The Vethea stage now also needs the Ancient Dramcryx (its spark goes into the Spirit of the Universe): Vethea mobs in the Overworld notice the player by the same rule.
- New config option worldGates (true by default). false - anyone walks through a lit portal and sleeps in the Nightmare Bed, as before; Arcana stays gated.
- On an existing server a player who is already in a world without the needed victory will be returned. If the victory was really there (for example, the boss was defeated before the addon counted it), an operator can grant it: /dreboss grant <boss> <player>.
## Fixed
- Xaero's Minimap: the Scorcher, Frost and Ayeraco summons are shown on the radar as dots, like the other summons, not as question marks.
## Other
