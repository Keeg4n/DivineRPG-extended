## Overworld weapon subtypes

Overworld and Nether swords, knives and mauls now differ by the target's invulnerability window: how long it takes the target to receive a new hit after the previous one. Normally this is 10 ticks, half a second.

| Subtype | Weapons | New hit after | Damage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Knife | Ocean Knife, Jungle Knife, Aquatic Dagger, Terran Dagger and Knife, Double Claw | 6 ticks (0.3 s) | x0.75 |
| Rapier | Rupee Rapier, Jungle Rapier, Arlemite Sword | 8 ticks (0.4 s) | x0.9 |
| Sword | All other Overworld and Nether melee weapons | 10 ticks (0.5 s) | x1 |
| Maul | Aquatic, Bedrock, Crab Claw, Furious, Corrupted, Terran, Aquatooth | 20 ticks (1 s) | x2 |

- A hit stronger than the previous one, as before, passes through inside the window by the difference.
- The window is shared by everyone who hits the target. The training dummy counts as a mob: it is handy for comparing weapons.
- The subtype and its numbers are shown in the weapon tooltip.
- Weapons from other worlds and other mods did not change, and neither did projectiles.

## Setting for administrators

The melee category in the add-on config:
- enabled: everything at once. false - all weapons as in regular Minecraft.
- pvp: the same rules in combat between players. Off by default: hits on players are as in regular Minecraft. If enabled: knife 8 ticks x1, rapier 9 ticks x1, sword unchanged, maul 20 ticks x1.5. Protected regions with PvP disabled are not bypassed.
- melee.knife, melee.rapier, melee.sword, melee.maul: each subtype has its own windowTicks and damage for mobs, and windowTicksPvp and damagePvp for players.
