## Vethea: the queue of the last bosses

- The queue Raglok - Wreck - Lady Luna is now enforced by gear rather than by a ban on counting the victory: Wreck is seriously hurt by Helio weapons (boulders from Raglok) and better ones, Lady Luna - by Arksiane (boulders from Wreck) and her own weapon.
- A weaker weapon barely hurts the boss, and a message says so.
- Wanderer perks: Raglok unlocks Regeneration, Wreck - Arcana Flow, Lady Luna - Angelic Flight.
