## Corrupt altar: enchantments visible in advance

- The Divine corrupt altar now works by the add-on's rules (setting corruptAltar; false - as in Divine). It still does not need bookshelves.
- The enchantments of each slot can be revealed in advance: there is a button with a price to the left of the slot. The first slot costs 1 corruption shard, the second 5 shards, the last a corruption stone (setting altarRevealCost). The payment goes into the cell next to the item and is free in creative mode. Hover over a slot to see the full list of revealed enchantments.
- What is revealed is exactly what will be applied to the item. If you take the item out and put it back, the prices and enchantments do not change and you do not need to pay a second time. New enchantments appear only after enchanting at the altar, as in 1.8.
- Enchanting is more expensive: the last slot costs 50 levels (setting altarTopLevels), and the first two cost the vanilla prices multiplied by 5/3 (3-17 and 10-35). In return, the roll power equals the price: a diamond sword can get Sharpness V, which a regular table does not give.
- The last slot has only the best enchantments: Protection, Feather Falling, Respiration, Aqua Affinity, Sharpness, Looting, Fire Aspect, Efficiency, Silk Touch, Fortune, Unbreaking, Power, Flame, Infinity, Luck of the Sea, Lure. On a diamond chestplate that is Protection IV and almost always Unbreaking III.
- A book no longer collects enchantments at random (Protection, Luck of the Sea and Sharpness on one book): an item is chosen first (an armor piece, sword, pickaxe, bow or fishing rod), and all the book's enchantments fit it. A book gets all the enchantments of the roll - a regular table gives only one.
- The enchantment level is the highest one the power allows. With a regular table, Protection disappears entirely if the power is too high; the altar does not do that.
- The altar cells work like the Wanderer's Anvil: if you close the window, leave the server or die, the item and the payment return to the inventory, and whatever does not fit drops at your feet.

## Selftests

- New "Corrupt altar" tests: prices and enchantments over 400 seeds (the rolls repeat, only the best enchantments are in the last slot, books fit one item); the window (payment with shards and a stone, a repeat view is free, enchanting with exactly the revealed enchantments, a book with all its enchantments, returning items, creative mode, shift-click); right-clicking the altar opens the add-on window, sneaking with a block places the block, and farther than 8 blocks the window closes.
