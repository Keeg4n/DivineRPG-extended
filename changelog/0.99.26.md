## Arcane Brewing Stand

- New Arcane Brewing Stand, sold by Vatticus. Fuel is Arcanium (one is enough for 4 brews); without it the stand does not brew.
- Water bottle + ingredient = arcane potion. Boosts: glowstone dust - stronger, redstone - longer, gunpowder - area (also affects players within 8 blocks).
- 16 arcane potions: Clarity, Sorcerer's Brew (arcane damage), Infusion of Composure (cheaper shots), Charm Ward, Stone Skin, Deflection, Elixir of Fortitude, Bramble Hide, Inner Heat (Iceika cold does not build up), Battle Fervor, Steady Hand, Arcane Hunger, Way Home, Call to Arms, Stillness, Fisher's Fortune.
- Divine's arcana potions now give the Clarity effect: no regeneration delay, arcana flows faster.
- Icons of active arcane potions are shown above the arcana bar. Effects survive rejoining, death removes them.
