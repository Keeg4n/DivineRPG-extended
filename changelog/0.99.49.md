## The Watcher's lair

- Without a fight the hall is empty: only broken columns and a dais with the eye.
- The netherrack rubble collapses on summoning the Watcher. When the Watcher has died or vanished (everyone left the arena), the rubble goes away and the knocked-down columns return to their places. What players have placed is not touched.
- In already created worlds the lair was generated with rubble. It will clear itself when a player enters the lair and the Watcher is not there.

## City under the jungle temple

- The Ancient Dramcryx arena is now in the center of the dome: round, paved, with columns and an altar, with a collapsed fence along the edge with four passages.
- Around the arena is a ring boulevard with lanterns, farther out the city blocks form a ring. The dome size is unchanged.
- The tower with the staircase from the temple stands in the city, west of the arena. From it the main street leads to the arena.
- New roots from the vault: they grow from the vault flaring out, narrow downward and curve off to the side in an arc, bark on all sides, with vines on them hanging lower than the ends. Some roots glow at the tip, some grow to the floor and spread into supports (only deep in the blocks, streets are free).
- Affects new chunks: already built cities stay as they were.
