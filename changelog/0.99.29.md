## Boss phases for arcana

- Dramix while invisible: regular damage x0.2, arcane x1.5.
- Parasecta at 75%, 50% and 25% health closes herself in a chitin cocoon: regular damage x0.1, arcane damage splits the cocoon - she is stunned and takes x1.5.
- Soul Fiend's souls die from arcana three times faster, and from regular damage twice as slowly.
- The Arcana Saber, after a regular hit, deals a separate arcane hit through armor, with purple sparks.
- NEI got an Arcane Brewing Stand page: the recipe of each potion and all boosts. Ingredients have a tooltip saying which potion is brewed from them.
