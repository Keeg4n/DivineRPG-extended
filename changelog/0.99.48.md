## The Watcher: a new lair and a new fight

### Lair
- A buried observatory under a Nether lava lake: an obsidian hall, lava above the vault, inside a rubble of netherrack, soul sand, brick fragments and columns of the old observatory.
- The entrance is a sloping tunnel from the shore with a pier. The lair map leads to the pier.
- On summoning the rubble is assembled anew, leaving free space around the players.
- Old observatories in already generated chunks remain.

### Fight
- The Watcher flies to the nearest player and blasts a path through the rubble with explosions.
- A beam stretches before the shot; a third of the charge before firing the aim freezes - if you managed to get around a corner, you dodged.
- After the shot it is overheated for 2 seconds and takes double damage.
- Below 50% health it shoots more often, below 20% netherrack boulders fall from the ceiling (lava trickles down before the fall).
- Explosions in the hall break only the rubble and drop nothing; obsidian, chests and items on the ground stay intact, the Watcher's own explosions do not hurt it.

### Weapons against the Watcher
- Ghast Cannon: a hit during charging disrupts the shot, stuns and overheats; its bullets hit 1.5 times harder.
- Fire weapons (Inferno, Molten swords, Flaming Fury, arrows of the Inferno bow): 4 hits in a row overheat it.
- Other Nether weapons: +50% damage.
- Bluefire bow: the arrow blinds for 3 seconds, its explosions also cut through the rubble.
