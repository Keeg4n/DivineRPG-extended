## Overworld lairs

- Lairs of one kind no longer stand right next to each other: at least 40 chunks (640 blocks) between them. Applies to Carcinus lairs, Glacon ice caves, tombs under pyramids and underground cities under jungle temples. Of two close ones one remains, an extra pyramid or temple is not placed at all. The distance is configurable (lairGap). Affects new chunks.

## Mortum

- The slopes around all Mortum arenas are covered with Mortum grass (before, bare dirt).
- Under the arenas near Lethe there are no more air pockets: voids at the world bottom under the arena are filled with dirt.
- Demonic Circle: the tower roof is a solid cone of dark brick with obsidian bands and a spire (before, sparse blocks with holes), the turret cones are solid too.

## Twilight dimensions

- Sacred groves: tree roots no longer hang from under the island as stalactite columns. Trunks end inside the island, and from below the island is braided with roots: from each tree they spread out, winding, toward the center, with short hanging tips at the ends.

## Items

- The Dream Wings Template and Midnight Wings Template were redrawn in the style of Divine templates: a light gray sheet with an embossed silhouette of wings.

## Easter egg

- A Serenade Striker thrown on the ground and struck by lightning becomes Musou no Hitotachi: a volumetric curved tachi with a glowing purple blade, a golden tsuba and a purple handle - in the hand and on the ground. All properties of the serenade remain. Lightning does not burn it.
