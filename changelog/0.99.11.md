## Fixes and improvements

### Apalachia Cart
- Rolls over the outer edges of a block: from the roof to the side face, down, under the bottom and back.
- On a wall and ceiling only the picture tilts, while the view for the game stays true: hits and clicks go where you look. The mouse turns together with the picture.

### Bosses and arenas
- Near a living boss (48 blocks) flight is disabled, except for Skythern and creative mode. Can be turned off with the `bossNoFly` setting.
- The Hall of Mirrors altar was moved to the center, the Gallery altars were moved away from the throne. On already built arenas the crystal summons Karot in the center of the hall, and the Eternal Archer on the dais in front of the throne.
- In the second encounter the Warrior King jumps onto the top of the mound instead of inside it.
- Clicking the Skythern Wind Totem again no longer resets the countdown; it resets only if you walk away from the totem.
- The Frost Archer shoots once every 1-2 seconds, like a skeleton, instead of 10 arrows per second.
- Carcinus got its own texture - a crab with a reddish tint (before, a foreign one was stretched over it and looked like mush).

### Structures (affects new ones)
- Frozen Throne: floor lamps in a grid every 5 blocks, rings of lamps in the pillars with stairs, lamps at the edge of the steps and on the pylon platforms. Mobs do not spawn in the Throne on their own at all, regardless of light.
- Dungeons, archers, ice lakes and Iceika trees are no longer placed inside the Frozen Throne.
- Over the whole Skythern temple island the sky is open all the way to the top of the world, and trees do not grow on the temple.
- Twilight Demon tower: removed the cavity under the courtyard, a passage was cut from the lower door to the foot of the path - the entrance is visible from below.

### Twilight dimensions and the End
- The portal frame that the teleport itself built on the other side (and the platform under it) drops nothing when mined. A portal built by a player breaks as usual.
- In the End a mob spawns if there is at least one player of the required rank nearby, even when a lower-ranked player is nearby. Such mobs do not attack the low-rank player.

### Items
- Planks from the four Vethea trees: Dreamwood, Hyrewood, Firewood, Mintwood (1 log - 4 planks). Everything that is made from regular planks can be crafted from them.
- All 24 fish textures were redrawn.
