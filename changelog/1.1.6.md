## New
- The addon's own melee enchantments. They come only from the corrupt altar (its rolls on melee weapons and its sword books); the enchanting table never offers them. Their multipliers stack with the weapon subtype (knife, rapier, sword, maul, Vethea claws and hammers) and with each other, and each one is described in the tooltip right under it.
  - Sweep: damage x0.75, but up to 4 mobs of the target's kind in front of you, within 3 blocks of the target, take the same hit. Players, villagers and tamed animals are never swept; the swept hits do not set off on-hit effects or the maul waves again.
  - Agility I-III: the target stays invulnerable after a hit x0.8 / x0.65 / x0.5 as long (never below 4 ticks), damage x0.85 / x0.72 / x0.6.
  - Might: damage x1.3, the target stays invulnerable x1.4 as long (at most 30 ticks) - like the Vethea hammers.
  - Crown: at full health and 10 s without taking damage, damage x1.4.
  - Spite: for 5 s after taking damage, the target stays invulnerable x0.7 as long.
  - Sweep, Agility and Might take the place of Sharpness, Smite, Bane of Arthropods and Wrath: one of them per weapon. If Sharpness is still put on top through the anvil, the strike enchantment stops working, and the tooltip says so in red. Crown and Spite - one of the two per weapon.
  - Against players they work only with melee.pvp.
  - Config category enchants: each one can be switched off (it is then not registered at all) and tuned; IDs 120-124 by default, a taken ID is replaced with a free one and written to the log.
## Testing
- Hold a sword: `/enchant <player> 120` Sweep, `/enchant <player> 121 <1-3>` Agility, `/enchant <player> 122` Might, `/enchant <player> 123` Crown, `/enchant <player> 124` Spite.
- A book: `/give <player> enchanted_book 1 0 {StoredEnchantments:[{id:121s,lvl:3s}]}` (id and lvl as above).
- Targets: `/summon Zombie ~2 ~ ~`, several in front of you for Sweep; a villager or a tamed wolf next to them must stay untouched.
