## Lair maps: locations from the world seed

### Overworld lairs
- The locations of the Carcinus lair, the ice cave and the Ancient Tomb are now computed from the world seed in advance. The lair is built when a player reaches the location. Affects new chunks: old parts of the world do not change.
- Carcinus lair: one location per 320 x 320 block square (`carcinusSpacing`), on a sandy beach by the sea.
- Ice cave: one location per 640 x 640 block square (`snowDungeonSpacing`), on flat snowy land.
- Ancient Tomb: exactly where the game places a desert pyramid.
- The Carcinus lair picks an entrance from several points at the water's edge and lowers the maze below the deep seabed, so it also spawns by steep Biomes O' Plenty shores.

### Maps
- A map leads to the nearest lair, built or future, even if the land there has not been generated yet. Locations where building failed are skipped.
- The search radius is 5000 blocks from the player (`mapSearchRadius`). A lair closer than 128 blocks (`mapSkipNear`) counts as found, and the map leads to the next one.
- The shared map cooldown is 5 seconds (`mapCooldownSeconds`); on an early click the map says how long to wait.
- In the creative menu and NEI, maps are listed in order of progression.

### Fixes
- Fixed a crash when opening the "Search" tab in the creative inventory.
- The Halite Pickaxe and Halite Multitool tooltip now reads: "Breaks any ore and stone of the Twilight dimensions, as well as jungle ore".
