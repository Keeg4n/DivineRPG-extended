## Fixed

- Wanderer armor and the H.E.V. suit: the item in hand follows the arms of the armor - the drawn bow is aimed forward, the sword moves with the swing. The game draws the item on the arm of the player's main model, and in some builds (other mods) that model stays in the rest pose: since 1.1.8 the armor arms moved while the bow or the sword hung still at the hip. Now the armor passes the pose of its arms to the main model before the item is drawn.
