## Stages: foreign mobs in the Overworld and bosses of a stage not yet reached

- Mobs of Iceika, the Twilight dimensions, Arcana and Vethea that end up in the Overworld (spawner, egg, portal) now look at the player's stage, as Overworld mobs look at rank: a player who has not reached their world is not chosen as a target and is not hurt, and if such a player killed them - no drops and no experience. You cannot bring a spawner and farm around the stage. At home these mobs behave as before; the rule does not touch merchants and tamed mobs.
- When a world counts as open: Iceika - after Carcinus, Eden and Arcana - after the Watcher, Wildwood, Apalachia, Skythern and Mortum - after the guardian of the previous world, Vethea - after the Spirit of the Universe bosses (three Mortum bosses, Dramix, Parasecta, Ancient Entity, King of Scorchers). Disabled by the foreignMobsByStage setting.
- Bosses in the Overworld, Nether and End: a player who has not reached the boss gets neither their share of the loot (shards, trophies, sparks, accessories) nor the victory - the chat says whom to defeat first. If none of those who hit it has reached it, nothing drops from the boss and there is no experience. The chain: Carcinus - Lich King and Snow Glacon - Ancient Entity, King of Scorchers and the jungle bosses - the Watcher; Twilight bosses, Arcana and Vethea bosses - as their worlds; the Ayeraco horde - after the Ancient Entity.
