## Wanderer's Anvil

- When the window is closed, the items from the slots fall on the ground in front of the player, like from the grid of a vanilla crafting table, instead of going into the inventory. The same applies on leaving the server, death, breaking the block or walking too far away.
- Switching tabs works as before: whatever does not fit the new tab goes to the inventory.

## Wanderer armor icons

- The piece icons are now flat, 16x16, like regular armor, instead of a render of the 3D model. Each tier has its own colors and details: the Eden halo, the Wildwood plus slit, the Apalachia feathers, the Skythern crest, the Mortum hood, the Halite spire and horns.

## Selftests

- The anvil tabs test now checks that after closing, the items lie on the ground, they are not in the inventory, and a second close adds nothing.
