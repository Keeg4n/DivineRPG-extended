## Changed
- Boss arenas got new looks (new chunks only: arenas and lairs that are already built stay as they are). Fight layouts are unchanged: altars, covers, daises, graves, pylons, platforms and radii stay where they were.
  - Karot - Black crystal: over the Hall of Mirrors a faceted obsidian pyramid with rows of purple glass and glowing edges, crystal shards grow from the ground around (none in front of the doors).
  - Densos and Reyvor - Sun and moon: over the west wall behind Densos a quartz sun with rays and lamps, over the east wall behind Reyvor an obsidian crescent with a netherite brick rim and stars; the rift glows from the bottom.
  - Eternal Archer - Basilica: a roof of netherite brick with an obsidian ridge of spikes and lamps, stepped buttresses with pinnacles at every pier, a rose window over the entrance, four towers with cones and a bell tower behind the throne.
  - Soul Fiend - Tree of souls: a huge dead tree instead of the mausoleum, the altar in its hollow (the opening to the south), roots between the graves, souls hanging on chains from the branches, glowing bone sprouts instead of the dry trees.
  - Eden, Great Cadillion - Sun sanctuary: a mosaic floor with twelve yellow rays, a ring of twelve quartz columns under an open ring architrave with sun discs, four obelisks, a pool, thin trees along the rim; the sky over the clearing is open.
  - Wildwood, Ancient Golem - Cromlech: the forest steps back to the island rim; an outer ring of ten trilithons with torches and a horseshoe of five tall trilithons open to the south, a ring path, the springs became ponds with lily pads. The mossy covers stay (boulders still crush them).
  - Apalachia, Warrior King - Ceremonial mound: round tiers with stone belts and wide stairs, a fire bowl in a ring of painted totems on the summit, tall totems, striped tents and fires below, a palisade with a walkway and towers at the gates.
  - Skythern, Wind Lord - Floating clouds: no colonnade; a wind rose on the floor, the totems stand on four floating cloud islands reached by spirals of cloud steps, eight twisted wind spires on the rim, a broken marble ring floating above, wind-bent trees.
  - Ayeraco horde - Nest: a woven rim of obsidian and quartz rods rising to the island edge, three claws on each tower, an egg spotted with the tower's color at each perch, twigs at the arena edge.
  - Ancient Dramcryx - Water garden: the arena is an island of grass and moss with twelve living jungle trees in place of the columns (the ram hits them), a stele behind the altar; the boulevard became a canal of still water with lamps underneath and four bridges, columns of water in glass from the floor to the vault.
  - Frozen Throne - Cathedral: a six-ray snowflake on the floor, an alley of obelisks to a much larger throne with a fan of ice spikes and two ice knights, crystal pylons with shards (the platforms are at the same height), the citadel with a pointed dome, a lantern and a crown of twelve spires.
  - Ancient Tomb - Clock hall: a round domed hall, the floor is a clock dial with the hour hand pointing to the altar, ten clock columns with lamps, a big hourglass behind the altar.
  - Carcinus - Ship graveyard: in the grotto the bow of a ship, a fallen mast with a lantern, crates and a fish skeleton; at the entrance a ship aground over the funnel, broken in the middle, with a torn sail and lanterns around.
- Lair decor that would be a free resource (planks, logs, sail, clay, sand of the wreck and the tomb) drops nothing, does not collapse the burrow and cannot be taken whole with silk touch.
- Frozen Throne: one of the twelve braziers around the center stands one step higher, on the new throne dais.
