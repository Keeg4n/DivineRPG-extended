## Accessories on the body, new icons and altars

- Accessories are visible on the body: everything embedded in the worn armor is drawn on the player model - rings at the wrist, pendants on a cord, brooches and medals on the chest, belts, pauldrons, a crown and circlets on the head, wings on the back, a quiver, scabbards and bags at the hip, hunter's marks at the temple and so on. Visible on yourself (in third person and in the inventory window) and on other players. The fit is calculated from the armor surface: on regular armor, without armor and on each of the 11 looks of Wanderer armor the item sits in its place. Can be disabled in the client settings (accessoriesOnBody).
- New icons for all accessories chosen by the tester (concepts of 08.11): 1B 2C 3B 4B 5B 6C 7A 8C 9A 10C 11A 12A 13A 14C 15B 16C 17A 18A 19C 20A 21A 22A (scarf with fit A1: the tail across the chest over the armor) 23A 24B 25A 26C 27A 29-32A 33B 34C 35A 36B, hunter's marks 37-47 - C. The Flying Carpet (28) stayed as it was and is not drawn on the body.
- Summoning altars in a new look: Carcinus - water in sandstone, Snow Glacon - a snow cap on lapis, Frozen Throne - quartz and ice, Ancient Tomb - sandstone with an ankh, the world guardian nest - overgrown emerald, the jungle lair - emerald in temple stone, the Mortum arena - redstone cracks in obsidian. The Ayeraco Horde Altar stayed as it was.
- The Arcane Ward and Crystal of Clarity icons are now their own (from the concepts), so they are no longer assembled from DivineRPG textures and are identical on any Divine version.
