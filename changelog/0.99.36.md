## Large Ayeraco Aerie

### Island
- The Aerie is now 1400 blocks from the center of the End, reached by 7 islets. In old worlds the previous small island remains as a respite, and the horde horn works only on the new one. Affects new chunks.
- The island is in the spirit of the chaos island from Draconic Evolution: radius about 48 blocks, a ragged edge, a drop below with obsidian icicles, a flat arena in the center with obsidian rings.
- Around the island is an asteroid belt of end stone.
- The color pillars stand on obsidian towers with a spiral staircase - you can walk up and break them on foot.

### Combat
- The fight: the horde circles high in a large circle, flies in one by one and stays close for 8 seconds.
- Attacks: a cone of fire breath (with a wind-up, turns slowly - you can step aside), a lobbed fireball with burning ground (does not break blocks), a point-blank bite. When three or fewer Ayeracos are left, they fly in two at a time.
- Fire deals fire damage, a Fire Resistance potion saves you.
- Summon beams no longer pile up on the island: when the horde has fallen or left the arena, all beams around the Aerie are removed.
