## New
- Note blocks play new instruments on the blocks of the twilight worlds and Vethea. As in vanilla, the block under the note block picks the instrument:
  - Eden Block - hypersaw (F#2-F#4);
  - Wildwood Block - electric guitar (F#3-F#5);
  - Apalachia Block - synth pluck (F#3-F#5);
  - Skythern Block - crystal bell (F#4-F#6);
  - Mortum Block - bass guitar (F#1-F#3);
  - Vethea dream stone, dream bricks (light, dark, red), luna stone and luna bricks - music box, a lullaby (F#4-F#6).
- The pitch is set with right clicks as usual (two octaves), the colored note particle is the same as in vanilla, the volume follows the "Jukebox/Note Blocks" slider. Works in singleplayer and on servers.
- The sounds are made from CC0 recordings on Freesound; the authors are listed in the README.
## Other
- Selftest: every instrument at the lowest, middle and highest step, plain stone still plays the vanilla harp, the sound files and sounds.json entries are in place.
