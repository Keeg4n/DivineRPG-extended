## Fixed

- Summons respect protected regions: in a region where PvP is off, a player or a peaceful creature (animals, villagers, pets) hit by the owner no longer becomes the summons' target, and a target that walks into such a region is dropped. This applies to all summons and to the ghost. Before, a hit on someone's cow in a protected region was enough to send the summons after it.
- Frost's ice shards hurt only Frost's target and hostile mobs in their way. They fly through animals, villagers, pets, other players and anything a protected region guards from the owner.

## Other

- Selftests for summons, Frost and the region checks were run.
