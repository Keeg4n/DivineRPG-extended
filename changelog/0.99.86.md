## Armor fork before Eden

The first tier of Wanderer armor can now be forged not only from Divine armor but from any of the five final Overworld sets. Whichever set you bring gives the R1 look, and its property stays in the armor as a core on all tiers. For bosses all five sets count as R1, and they no longer get the x3 penalty at the Eden Cadillion.

| Set | Forging fee | Core |
|---|---|---|
| Divine | 16 realmite ingots, as before | +6 melee damage |
| Shadow | 8 divine stones | speed x3 |
| Bedrock | 12 divine stones | fire does not hurt, explosions do not hurt after defeating the Watcher |
| Ender | 8 divine stones | void step with Ender weapons, explosions do not hurt after defeating the Watcher |
| Corrupted | 8 divine stones | projectiles hit 20% harder |

- The fee is needed because the sets themselves cost very different amounts: the Divine one needs 216 shards from bosses, while Bedrock is made of 72 rupee ingots and 56 obsidian. Numbers in the config: coreForgeStones, coreShadowSpeed.
- The core can be changed in the anvil: a Wanderer set on top, another final set in the donor slot, and its fee in the fuel slot. If the fuel is arlemite, the same set applies a regular perk.
- Old Wanderer armor counts as Divine: it now has +6 melee damage.

## New looks of the first tier

Four new models in the style of the rest of the Wanderer armor, each with its own flat icons:
- Shadow: sleek black plate armor, a closed helmet with a white slit, little wings at the temples, thin plates trailing back.
- Bedrock: speckled rock, wide pauldrons, a closed helmet with a rough-hewn crown.
- Ender: almost black plate armor with stars and glowing blue lines, eyes like an enderman's, an End crystal on the back.
- Corrupted: a wide-brimmed hat, eyes in shadow, a bandolier, a quiver with glowing arrows.

## New perks

Sets that previously passed nothing on now have a perk for Wanderer armor:
- Shadow: Light step, speed x1.25.
- Terran: Miner, Haste II.
- Corrupted: Marksman, projectiles x1.1.
- Arlemite: Missile ward, projectiles against you x0.75.
- Rupee of any color: Firm stance, mob hits x0.75.
