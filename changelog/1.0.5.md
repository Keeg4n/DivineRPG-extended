## Added

- Sound of Carols summons Frost: right-click into the air calls a flying snowflake instead of shooting notes. It is the only ranged summon: it does not rush at the target but keeps circling over your head and once a second shoots an ice shard at a target it can see. A shard deals 4 arcane damage and slows the target for 2 seconds; there are no explosions and blocks are not broken. Shards fly through you, your summons and players you cannot fight. 10 Arcana per second, attacks enemies within 12 blocks. Summoning rules are the same as for the other summons.
