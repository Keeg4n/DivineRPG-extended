## Pet Whistle and a batch of fixes from the sheet

### New
- Pet Whistle (iron ingot + gold nugget + string). Right-click - all your pets jump to you and drop their target. Hitting your own pet with the whistle releases it: pets from eggs return as an egg together with their name and health.
- Pets of one owner no longer fight each other.
- The Water Lure Torch shows a timer: click a burning torch - the chat says how much longer it will burn.

### Changes
- The Ayeraco Aerie was moved to 850 blocks diagonally from zero - the whole island with the asteroid belt is inside a 900 square on X and Z. It is reached by 4 islets.
- Fast teleports (void step, void rescue, Ender Scepter, Teleportation Crystal) on servers with plugins ask protected regions for permission; in a region that forbids teleporting there is none, and arcana and durability are not spent.
- Long item descriptions wrap by word, and in NEI the add-on's page texts wrap too.
- The "Hit me" label above the dummy is visible only when aimed at.
- The End armor description shows the time until the next void rescue and the full list of weapons for the void step.

### Fixes
- The dummy is removed only by the player's own hand hit (sneak + left click); pet damage while the owner is sneaking no longer removes it.
- Reduced server load: items, projectiles, blocks and bosses no longer load chunks for checks and check themselves less often every tick.
