## Fixed

- Ice blasts no longer destroy items and experience lying on the ground: the Fractite's exploding shot in Iceika, the Frost Charge of the Snow Glacon and the Lich King, and the Frost Colossus' ice boulder. Before, every dropped item and experience orb within about 6 blocks of a Fractite shot was wiped out - mob drops in a fight and the things of a player who died there. These blasts still never break blocks. Other explosions in Iceika (TNT and the like) work as before; boss loot was already safe.

## Other

- Selftest for ice blasts was run: a plain explosion of the same strength destroys an item and an orb, the three ice projectiles leave them.
