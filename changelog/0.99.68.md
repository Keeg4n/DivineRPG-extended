## Wanderer's Anvil: tabs

- The window is split into three tabs: "Upgrade" (Wanderer set, donor set, fuel), "Sockets" (armor and accessories) and "Discharge" (weapons). Each tab shows only its own slots, every row is labeled, and the main button is named for what it does: "Upgrade", "Embed", "Discharge". The last tab is remembered; when the anvil is opened with items in it, the tab that fits those items is selected.
- Armor slots go by piece: helmet in the first, boots in the last, and an empty slot shows the silhouette of its piece (as in the player inventory). The same applies to the donor set. Shift-click sorts items by itself: armor into the slot of its piece, an accessory into a free accessory slot, a weapon on the discharge tab.
- When you switch tabs, everything that does not fit the new tab goes to the inventory. If there is no room, the tab does not change, the items stay in the anvil, and a chat message says so.
- "Sockets" tab: an accessory from the bottom row is shown in the socket it will go into, faded and in a gold frame, before the button is pressed; the socket tooltip says "will go here". For armor without sockets (vanilla, other mods) it says outright that it has no sockets.
- "Discharge" tab: how much is loaded out of how much, and with what, and how to load (sneak + right-click with the weapon).
- Help is the "?" button next to the tabs: a panel over the window for the current tab, the text wraps to the screen width and is split into pages (click, space, arrows, wheel; Esc closes). The upgrade help lists the R1 recipes, the R2-R7 tempering, and all donor perk sets with the bosses you need to defeat. Before, all of this was one huge tooltip on "Donor (?)" that went off the edge of the screen at a large GUI scale.
- The status line is up to 4 lines (it used to be cut off at the second), and the texts are shorter and tell you what to do next.

## Divan RPG

- The RPG-7 no longer sinks into the seat, back and armrest: it lies on the seat cushions on its side, grips toward the edge, slightly slanted, between the armrests. The model on the divan is scaled down to 1.4 blocks - like a real RPG-7 with a grenade (before, it was as long as the whole divan). The position was calculated from the model vertices: none of them goes inside the divan.
