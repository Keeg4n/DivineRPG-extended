## Arcana: detonator and bosses

- Arcana Detonator: bullets converge on the aim point (a mob under the crosshair, a block, or a point 32 blocks away). Divine scattered not the direction but the start point of the bullets (+-1.5 blocks), and 30 bullets flew as a parallel cloud 3 blocks wide: the target caught only part of it at any distance, and bullets that started in a nearby wall died immediately. Now at point-blank range all of them hit the target, and each hit hurts; a bullet obstructed by a block starts from the eyes.
- Arcana bosses (Dramix, Parasecta) are hurt only by arcana and explosions. A regular hit does not go through at all, with no knockback; the attacker gets a chat hint. Dramix while invisible: arcana x1.5, explosion x0.2. Parasecta in a cocoon: explosion x0.1, arcana splits the cocoon.
- A Merik rocket fired near an Arcana boss targets it immediately: even an invisible Dramix, and even if another mob or player stands closer. Before, it took the nearest living target and did not change it while that one was alive.
