## Frostbite from ice weapons

- Ice weapons freeze mobs: the first hit slows (Slowness I for 3 s), the second slows more (Slowness III), and on the third the mob freezes into the ice for 2.5 s: it stands still, does not hit or shoot (its hits and projectiles do not hurt), a translucent ice block surrounds it, and it melts toward the end.
- Which weapons: Snow Venom, Ice Sword (both the Iceika and the Overworld one), Ender Rice, Snowslash, the Glacier and Royal ice swords, the Frozen Maul, Frossivence; the Icicle Bow and the Blizzard Bow; the Cold, Fractite and Ice Claw cannons; the Snow Shuriken.
- Stages build up if hits come no less often than once per 4 s; a hit more often than once per 0.4 s does not add a stage, so rapid-fire weapons do not freeze instantly.
- A thawed mob does not freeze again for 5 s - only a strong slowdown. Ice weapons only slow bosses, and do not touch players and pets.
- The tooltip of ice weapons has a line about frostbite. Disabled by the iceFreeze setting.
