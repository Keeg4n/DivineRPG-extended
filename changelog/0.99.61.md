## Easter egg

- Musou no Hitotachi - final touches from the in-game model:
  - the blade is pale pink-lilac, the edge almost white, along the body are purple flames with light veins; the guard has a thin neck, the tip is long and sharpened toward the spine;
  - the guard is dark with bronze trim: two spikes forward on the spine side, a long spike back on the blade side, above the blade a glowing purple curl (instead of a round emblem);
  - the handle is longer, dark, with bronze filigree and rings, in the middle a red crisscross wrap, two pink stones;
  - the pommel is bronze claws and a glowing pink stone.
- The blade, curl and pommel stone glow; the handle is still in the palm; the icon was redrawn from the model.
