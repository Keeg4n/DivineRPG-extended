## Halite tools and pickaxe levels

- The Halite Pickaxe and Halite Multitool rank above the Mortum pickaxe: they break any ore and stone of the Twilight dimensions.
- The Divine pickaxe-axe combo tool (shickaxe) breaks the same blocks as the Divine pickaxe.
- Fixed the tooltip of the Mortum set "Darkness Devourer": a hit has a 20% chance (35% in darkness) to heal for 30% of the damage, up to 1.5 hearts.
