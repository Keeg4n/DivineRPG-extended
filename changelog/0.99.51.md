## The Watcher

- In the second phase (rams) projectiles deal only 15% damage to it - you have to hit it point-blank.
- The best weapon against it at close range is Ender swords of any color and the Bedrock sword and maul: double damage.

## Entrance to the Nether lairs

- The gate is now cut into the sheer wall of a rocky hill: obsidian pylons with glowstone, quartz columns, the lair's crest above the gate. In front of the gate is a short porch. In rock it is simply an entrance in a cliff, over open lava - a rocky islet with piled walkways. The large dug courtyard around the gate was removed.

## King of Scorchers' Fire Chamber

- The chamber was moved under a lava lake, like the Watcher's lair: the same size, shell, windows with lava, oculus and entrance. The sphere in the sky is gone.
- Inside is a racing track (king - racer): in the center a dais in Divine's black-and-white checkerboard, around it a lava moat with four checkered walkways, on the track white dashed markings, a checkered finish line and a red-and-white curb, six checkered posts with eternal fire. Above the gate is a checkered flag.
- In already created worlds the old sphere chamber remains and works: the flame summons the king there, the map leads to it.

## Both Nether lairs

- Ghasts and other Nether mobs do not spawn on their own inside the halls.
- Explosions in the Scorchers' chamber do not break blocks either.
