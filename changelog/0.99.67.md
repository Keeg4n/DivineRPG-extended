## Accessories are built into armor

- Accessory slots are gone: an accessory works while the armor piece it is built into is worn. Sockets exist on any Divine armor (one in each piece) and on Wanderer armor (one in each piece, a second one for the boss of that piece: helmet - Karot, chestplate - Lich King, leggings - the Watcher, boots - Twilight Demon). Vanilla armor and armor from other mods have no sockets.
- Each accessory is built into its own armor piece:
  - helmet (head and neck): Lich Crown, Watchful Eye, Shell of the Deep, Dungeon Lamp, Jack's Pumpkin Amulet, Dead Man's Amulet, Warm Scarf, Crystal of Clarity, Hunter's Marks, Miner's Amulet;
  - chestplate (torso, shoulders, arms, back): Carcinus Shell, Colossus Pauldron, Ice Brooch, Ancient Charm, Pharaoh's Ankh, Medal of Valor, Arcane Ward, Seal of Dramix, Parasecta Sting, rings (Bone Archer Ring, Wither Ring, Ring of Eternal Ice), Dream Wings;
  - leggings (belt): Creeper Tamer Belt, Heiva Belt, Master Scabbard, Endless Quiver, Dust Pouch, Thrower's Sling, Tackle Box, Pocket Magnet;
  - boots (feet and movement): Flying Carpet, Ayeraco Wing, Blue Flame;
  - combined ones - into a piece of any of their components (Steadfast Belt - leggings or chestplate, Heart of Winter and Tide Armor - helmet or chestplate).
- Building in and taking out is done by the Wanderer's Anvil: the armor piece goes into the armor slot, the accessory into the donor, the "Embed" button. The price is experience levels: 10 (Jack's orders, finds from chests and mobs, cheap merchant items), 15 (boss loot, Hunter's Marks, expensive merchant items), 20 (combined ones, Flying Carpet, Ayeraco Wing, Dream Wings, Pharaoh's Ankh). Taking out is free: click the socket under the armor piece, and the accessory comes out into a free slot of the donor.
- There is no inventory inside the armor - only a record of what is built in; inserting and taking out work like crafting, so there is nothing to dupe with. The same effect is not built into one piece twice (nor a combined one together with its own part).
- Accessories always stay in the armor: on death they go with it, nothing is moved into the inventory.
- The anvil does not accept donor armor with built-in accessories (the upgrade would eat them - take them out first). When Realmite becomes Wanderer R1 armor, the accessories move to the new pieces. If armor with accessories was eaten by regular crafting or a vanilla anvil, the accessories are returned to the player.
- The accessory tooltip shows where it is built in and how much it costs; the armor tooltip shows its sockets and what is in them. Right-click with an accessory - the same tooltip in chat.
- Old accessory and ammo slots: nothing works in them anymore and nothing can be put in, items can only be taken out. The star button in the inventory is visible while anything is left there.
- /dreinv shows what is built into a player's worn armor, and opens the old slots if anything is left in them.

## Ammo is loaded into the weapon itself

- Ammo slots are gone. Sneak and right-click with a bow, phaser, blitz, cannon or La Vekor - everything suitable from the inventory goes inside, up to 4 stacks, with a reload sound (bows, dust and cannons each have their own). If there is nothing to load or nowhere to load it, sneak + right-click is a regular shot.
- A shot takes from the load first, then from the inventory - even with a full backpack, without touching anything in it. The load is visible in the weapon tooltip and to the right of the hotbar.
- Only a number is stored inside the weapon: items with their own data (renamed and so on) are not loaded. Unload at the Wanderer's Anvil: weapon in the fuel slot, the "Unload" button (into the inventory, as much as fits). If the weapon breaks, the load spills out to the player rather than vanishing.

## Selftest

- Tests of accessory and ammo slots were replaced with tests of sockets and load: sockets by armor kind and boss, only worn armor, one effect per piece, building in for levels and taking out, donor armor and the transition to R1, death, return from crafting and from the vanilla anvil, old slots - take only; the load, a shot from the load first, a full backpack, capacity, unloading, breaking, the cannon.
- The Vethea mob weakness test counts only its own loot: Magic modpack mods sometimes add their own random loot to a mob, and the test failed falsely.
