## Arcana: damage, prices and mages

- Weapon arcane damage goes through armor. If the target has arcana, 60% of the hit drains arcana (2 per point of damage), and the rest hits health; without arcana - entirely to health.
- Arcane damage grows with progression stage: from x1.0 on entering Arcana to x2.2 after Lady Luna.
- Prices and cooldowns of arcane weapons were recalculated (in Divine the weapon fired every tick without a pause): Firefly 4 arcana with a 0.3 s cooldown, the Starlight Staff 15, Angelic Flight 6 arcana per second instead of 20, regeneration delay 2 s.
- If there is not enough arcana, there is no shot, arcana is not spent and the delay is not reset.
- A volley of several projectiles hits with each projectile. Stars and meteors fall from a lower height and arrive faster.
- Twilight dimension mages shoot no more often than once per 1-1.5 s and only when they see the target, and withstand arcane damage twice as well. Arcana mobs in melee hit with arcane damage.
- Twilight dimension mages occasionally drop their staff (Mage, Spellbinder, Mystic, Sorcerer).
- Full Korma set: shots are 20% cheaper and +50 to the arcana pool. Storm Sword lightning does not hit its owner.
- Arcana potions give not instant arcana but a regeneration effect without delay. The arcana bar is not reset on rejoining.
- Instead of potions, Vatticus sells the accessories Arcane Ward (arcana absorbs 75% of arcane damage, 1 per point) and Crystal of Clarity (delay 0.75 s, regeneration +50%). Arcana merchant prices were recalculated.
- The arcane weapon tooltip shows the price, arcane damage and cooldown.
