## World mini-bosses

In the Twilight dimensions and on Vethea floors 1-3, mini-bosses appear: tougher than regular mobs but weaker than guardians, each with its own move. Their name and health are visible overhead.

- Eden, Noon Cori (225 health): hovers over the target and focuses a sunbeam, visible for 2 seconds. If the target is under open sky - damage, ignition and +15 overheating. A step under a roof saves you. When wounded it calls two Cori.
- Wildwood, Old Verek (150): at two thirds and one third health it calls a pack, and it leaps from afar. In a downpour it is invisible, and only a trail on the ground remains.
- Apalachia, Stonebreaker (300): stone skin holds 70% of damage. It is removed for 10 seconds by a maul or mace hit and by a boulder that falls on it. It summons boulders on the target itself.
- Skythern, Sky Grabber (180): dives, grabs and carries up 15 blocks, then drops onto the same spot. Knockback protection 1.0 does not let it lift you, and a strong hit makes it let go.
- Mortum, Mourner (400): each mob death within 16 blocks heals it and strengthens it, up to 5 times. It calls a retinue itself, and it is worthwhile to lead them away and kill them farther off.
- Vethea, Sleepwalker (200, 300, 400 by floor): the vulnerable weapon type changes every 20 seconds, and the spark color hints which. x2 to its own type, x0.25 to the others.

Loot in the Twilight dimensions: 2-3 world stones, 3-5 souls, sometimes a world chunk. The Sleepwalker drops boulders of its floor.

They appear no more than once per 20 minutes per player (setting miniBossMinutes, to disable - miniBosses), 20-32 blocks from them, not near world torches and not next to a boss. They do not affect progression. For testing: /dreminiboss.
