## Big update: bosses, weapons, fishing and crop beds

### Bosses
- Minions of add-on bosses and of bosses empowered by the add-on drop no loot or experience and die together with the boss (or after a few seconds if the boss disappears).
- Carcinus: claws reach 3.5 blocks - you can approach him with a sword; no more than 12 summoned crabs at once, and after a crab dies he does not summon new ones for 15 s. The grotto arena is twice as wide (affects new lairs).
- The flight ban applies only near Divine and add-on bosses; bosses from other mods do not prevent flying.
- Summon eggs of all new bosses are in the "Miscellaneous" tab of creative mode.

### Weapons
- Nether weapons (the Nether, Molten, Scorching and Blue Fire swords and the Nether Bow) hit frost mobs for x2 and set them on fire. Frost mobs: Glacons, Frosts, hostile Iceika mobs, Snow Glacon, Frost Colossus, Lich King and the Throne's dead.
- The Scorching Sword sets the target on fire for 4 s.
- The Ice Sword, Snow Venom and Ender Rice hit the Frost Colossus for x1.3, and the Throne's dead for x2.
- Frossivence no longer heals without a cooldown in combat.
- Red Ender Sword: an Enderman struck by a hit does not teleport while burning.
- Add-on weapon and armor effects do not affect players and peaceful creatures inside a protected region with PvP disabled (WorldGuard).
- Gear dropped by Divine mobs (for example, the Nether Bow from Wildfire) drops intact.
- In Vethea, protection from foreign weapons applies only to Vethea mobs; vanilla mobs and mobs from other mods take damage as usual.

### Lair maps
- Lairs are shown only within the `mapWorldSize` square centered on zero (10000 by default, that is, from -5000 to +5000).
- All maps in all worlds skip an already found lair and lead to the next one.
- Carcinus lairs are no closer than 256 blocks to each other. Affects new chunks.
- Fixed a map disappearing when opened from the last item in a stack; the map name is no longer italic.

### Fishing and fluids (affects new chunks)
- Ponds on the islands of Eden, Wildwood, Apalachia and Skythern: lily pads, a pier and a willow made from the world's wood (`twilightPondRarity`).
- Lakes in the grass on Vethea floors (`vetheaLakeRarity`).
- Lethe from a bucket flows like water and gives an infinite source; the Mortum river stays in its channel.

### Torches
- Torches repel Divine monsters of their own world only; in the overworld the skeleton torch repels everything, and the water torch repels aquatic mobs.
- New Nether Torch made from an ifrit rod, works in the Nether.

### Crop beds and items
- Dream Carrots are planted on farmland, and with Fortune the harvest is bigger.
- Dream Melon Seeds from a slice, the Dream Melon block grows on a stem.
- Snowballs stack to 64, the Rime Pickaxe breaks 3x3 and Rime Ore, and the Snow Ball tooltip names its source.
- Sticks of eight wood types - in the shape of a vanilla stick, in the color of their planks.

### Rails and cart
- A cart at full speed on Arcana Rails no longer falls through blocks at outer edges.
- A mob in a cart on a wall and ceiling is drawn rotated together with it; in creative mode a broken cart disappears.
- Service rail blocks are hidden from NEI; for Arcana and Apalachia rails NEI shows how to obtain them.

### Other
- Carcinus burrow blocks cannot be broken, and in the inventory they turn into regular sandstone, sand and chiseled sandstone.
- In Apalachia, ruins, camps and dungeons on the islands are twice as frequent (`apalachiaStructureBoost`).
