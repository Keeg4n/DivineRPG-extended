## World reliquaries

- Reliquaries appeared in the Twilight dimensions: locked chests that need the key of their world, each holding a world item, stones, souls and world dust. Unbreakable, like Arcana chests; the key is consumed.
- World key: any Arcana key in the middle of the crafting table and 4 world stones in a cross (Eden, Wildwood, Apalachia, Skythern, Mortum). The icon is the Divine key recolored to the world color at launch.
- Where they stand: Eden, Wildwood, Apalachia - in ruins on island tops, next to the ruin chest; Mortum - in a crypt by the sarcophagus; Skythern - on thorn ships.
- Thorn ships: they hang in the Skythern sky above the islands and between them. A hull of Skythern planks with a keel, two masts with white sails, a pennant, a cabin at the stern with a loot chest and (with a chance) a reliquary. They are found about once per 200 chunks.
- Reliquary chance: 1 in 12 in ruins and crypts, 1 in 3 on ships. Guarantee by 2048 x 2048 plots: if a plot does not yet have a reliquary, the 8th suitable spot (on ships - the 3rd ship) gets it without a roll. Only in new chunks.

## Items from reliquaries

- Eden - Sun Halo (accessory, head): by day under the open sky it heals half a heart every 2 seconds; Eden overheating does not grow with it. On the body - a golden ring above the head.
- Wildwood - Storm Bow: shoots like the Wildwood bow (Wildwood arrows, damage 6-16), does not break. In a downpour (and in any rain) every arrow strikes with lightning, otherwise every 5th; a hit with a storm arrow is lightning on the target and +25% damage, a miss is lightning where the arrow stuck.
- Apalachia - Tomahawk: a hatchet made of Apalachia material, does not break. Right-click - a throw up to 24 blocks: it hits up to 3 targets in a row (from the first to the nearest next one) and returns to the hand; while it flies, it cannot be thrown a second time. Thrown farther than 8 blocks, it stuns the first target for 2.5 seconds. It hits players only when PvP is on, and does not touch your own pets.
- Skythern - Cloud Staff: right-click - a 3 x 3 cloud platform for 30 seconds under your feet (catches a fall), and if you stand on the ground - 3 blocks ahead (a bridge across a chasm). Landing on a cloud does not hurt. Cooldown 40 seconds, a bar on the item.
- Mortum - Soul Harvest: a sword made of Mortum material, does not break. Each kill gives a soul (up to 10), each soul +2% damage; without kills the souls dissipate one per 10 seconds. At a full supply the next hit spends all souls on an explosion: half the hit damage to all mobs around (radius 4, through armor), which does not touch players and tamed mobs.
- Settings (the worldgen section): reliquaries, reliquaryChance, reliquaryPity, reliquaryShipChance, reliquaryShipPity, reliquaryRegion, skythernShipRarity.

- "From Vethea" in the tooltip of Vethea items is visible without Shift - as a line above "Shift - description".
- NEI: add-on armor goes in full sets - for Wanderer armor R1 with each core, then R2-R7 (helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots in a row), for angler armor too, in piece order. Divine armor in NEI already goes in sets.
