## Wanderer armor starts with Divine armor

- Wanderer armor R1 is now made from the Divine armor set: in the Wanderer's Anvil, "Upgrade" tab, the Divine armor set goes on top, no donor is needed, and the fuel is 16 realmite ingots (setting upgradeR1FuelRealmiteIngots). Divine armor already counts as tier R1, so defeating the Ancient Entity is no longer required for this step.
- Realmite and elite realmite no longer turn into Wanderer armor. Wanderer armor that is already made does not change.
- Accessories embedded in Divine armor carry over to the Wanderer pieces of the same type.
- If something is in the donor slot, the anvil asks you to remove it. The window texts and the "?" help were rewritten for the new first step.

## Wanderer armor looks

- Each tier of Wanderer armor has its own look: a 3D model on the character and its own helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots icons (before, all the armor looked like elite realmite).
  - R1: consecrated armor - gold and silver, a scarlet cape, a redstone gem, a golden hood with little wings.
  - R2 Eden: golden and scarlet petals, a golden circlet, a halo glowing above the head.
  - R3 Wildwood: plate armor made of blue wood, a closed helmet with branching horns, roots at the boots.
  - R4 Apalachia: orichalcum armor - crimson faceted plates, pink crystals, petals circling around, a faceted hood.
  - R5 Skythern: gray plate armor, a white hood and a cape with a light blue trim.
  - R6 Mortum: a black tattered cape and hood, red eyes glowing in the dark, chains on the belt.
  - R7 Halite: chlorophyte armor - green leaf plates, gray horns, a glowing leaf crystal hovering above the head.
- Glowing details (gems, halo, crystals, eyes) also glow in the dark. Each armor piece draws its own details, so you can wear pieces of different tiers.

## Wanderer's Anvil model

- The anvil is no longer a cube: dark iron with a horn and a polished work surface, copper realmite bands, a hammer lying on the surface, and glowing purple socket stones at the front and back. It is placed facing the player. The same model is used in hand, on the ground and in the inventory.
- The anvil no longer blocks light.

## The anvil stores nothing

- The anvil slots now work like a crafting table grid: each open window has its own, and nothing lies in the block or in the world. Close the window and everything returns to the inventory; whatever does not fit drops at your feet. The same happens on leaving the server, death, walking away from the anvil and breaking the block.
- Two players at one anvil no longer share slots: each has their own window.
- Items left in anvils from older versions go to the first player who opens the anvil (an entry is written to the admin log), or drop if the anvil is broken.
- The anvil no longer updates every tick.

## Fixes

- In the anvil help, five perks showed an internal string instead of the donor set name: Satiety (Skeleman), Night Reaper (Jack o'Man), Regeneration (Vemos), Arcana Flow (Korma), Angelic Flight (Angelic).

## Selftests

- The test "Anvil: upgrade does not eat accessories" was rewritten for the new step: Divine armor without a donor and without the Ancient Entity becomes Wanderer R1, realmite is not accepted, an extra donor is refused, accessories carry over, the fuel is deducted, and the skin is the R1 look.
- New test "Wanderer: tier looks": every tier has a model with all four pieces, an atlas and icons, and the item skin matches its tier.
- The test "Anvil: tabs" now also checks closing the window (everything returned, a second close does nothing) and handing out old items from the block (once, and they are removed from the block). The other anvil tests work with the window's working slots instead of the block.
