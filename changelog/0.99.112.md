## World fishing

- Eden - golden catch: any fish in Eden sometimes comes gilded (8%, with the Sun Lure 16%). A gilded fish glitters; Leorna pays triple for it, and eating it also gives Haste II for a minute.
- Sun Lure: a new bait made of 2 Eden sparks and a gold nugget (2 pieces). Strength 35, in Eden a golden catch is twice as frequent.
- Wildwood - Storm Catfish: caught only in a downpour (and in rain), with bait. A fish for Leorna's quests.
- Skythern - cloud islands: cloud islands with a pond in the middle hang in the Skythern sky (about once per 70 chunks). Fishing in such a pond is its own zone: cloudfins, a Cloud Jelly (for Leorna), souls, Skythern stones and pieces, feathers, its own crate. Thorn ships with a loot chest - since 0.99.111.
- Mortum - soul fishing: in Mortum (in Lethe and in water) a Wandering Soul can be caught. Right-click - gives 3 souls to Soul Harvest from the inventory. With bait, sometimes (3%) instead of a catch a drowned one surfaces from the water - a Mortum mob that attacks immediately.
- World gear in crates: in the fishing crates of the Twilight dimensions there is a 2% chance of a weapon or tool of that world (blade, slicer, blitz, phaser, bow, pickaxe, axe, shovel), and a 0.5% chance of a piece of world armor. In NEI this is visible on the crate page.
- Schools: sometimes a bubbling spot appears on the water not far from the player for a minute. A bobber in the spot gets bites three times faster and rare catches one and a half times more often. Disabled by the fishSchools setting.
- Golden Fish: in any Twilight dimension 1 in 1000 bites, and neither strength, nor bait, nor rod affect it. Eating it gives +1 heart permanently (survives death), up to 5 in total.
- Rare world fish for Leorna's quests: Sun Perch (Eden), Storm Catfish (Wildwood), Amethyst Pike (Apalachia), Cloud Jelly (Skythern cloud ponds), Bonefish (Mortum).
- Settings: cloudIslandRarity (worldgen), fishSchools (fishing).
- Hotfix: released without running the selftests, not checked in game.
