## Divine armor, Skythern weightlessness, Mortum afterlife

### Divine armor
- The melee bonus is now only against enemies: +6 against hostile mobs and players, +10 against undead, none against peaceful and neutral ones. The set and the core work the same.

### Skythern
- Full Skythern set: instead of a high jump, weak gravity. Jumps are long, falling is twice as slow, and there is no fall damage. When sneaking you fall as usual.
- A mob hit pushes like a Wind Lord gust. One mob pushes no more than once every 4 seconds, and knockback protection holds. You can now be knocked off the island: setting skythernGust.
- Ground mobs are also in the weak pull and jump in an arc toward a target that is higher or farther than 6 blocks, up to 16 blocks. Archers, bosses and minions do not jump.

### Mortum
- A mob killed by a player has a 25% chance to leave a soul for 3 seconds (setting mortumSoulChance). Hit it - it dissipates and leaves a world soul. Miss it - the mob rises with 40% health, with speed, already without loot and experience, and does not rise a second time.
- A full Mortum set collects the soul by itself from 4 blocks: a world soul and 1 heart.
- Bosses, their minions and Soul Fiend spawn leave no soul.
