## New Wanderer armor looks

- All seven tiers were redone with slim proportions: the armor follows the body, the chest narrows into a waist, and gaps are visible on the arms and legs. Before, the armor looked bloated.
- The capes are gone, because they conflicted with capes on the server. Instead, there is an item on the back that fits the theme of the world.
- The armor piece icons are new as well.

## By tier

- R1 Divine: golden plate armor with steel inlays, an open helmet, and a silver shield with a golden cross on the back.
- R2 Eden: petals and a halo.
- R3 Wildwood: plate armor of blue planks, a closed helmet with a plus-shaped slit through which the skin shows, branch horns, and a staff on the back.
- R4 Apalachia: a fan of feathers as a headdress, beads, fringe, and a fan of feathers on the back.
- R5 Skythern: a closed helmet with a crest, white plate armor with a sky-colored trim, little wings on the heels, and folded wings on the back.
- R6 Mortum: a deep hood, red eyes in a dark arch, and a bone spine on the back.
- R7 Halite: chlorophyte armor, a closed helmet with a spire, a black plus with red eyes, twisted horns, scales, fur, claws, and three sharp leaves on the back.
