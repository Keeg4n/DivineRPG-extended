## Locked Arcana chests

- Locked chests for the four Divine keys appeared in Arcana: the Sludge Chest, the Ancient Chest, the Soul Chest and the Degraded Chest. They open with their own key in hand (right-click): the key is consumed, loot appears inside, and after that it is a regular chest. A foreign key does not fit.
- Where they stand: in the closets behind key doors, where Divine puts arcanite ore (behind slime, ancient brick and soul stone doors). The chest takes the place of the ore, so the passage does not get narrower (if the closet is at the west or north edge of the chunk - in the adjacent cell of the same closet, and the ore stays: this way generation does not pull in the neighboring chunk). The degraded doors have no ore closets: this is a cross-shaped room with four degraded doors leading to its center, and the chest stands in a corner of the central hall, off to the side of the passage. Divine doors did not change, they still open with redstone too.
- A chest is not placed in every such closet but with a chance of 1 in 20. Guarantee: the Arcana world is divided into 2048 x 2048 plots (any 10000 x 10000 square contains at least nine whole ones). If a plot does not yet have a chest of some kind, the 12th suitable closet of that kind gets a chest without a roll. Only in new chunks.
- Loot: slime - consumables (Arcana potions, add-on arcane potions, collectors, sometimes a charged collector, grenades, arcanium, tokens); ancient - one Arcana weapon; soul - an accessory (Dungeon Lamp, Arcane Ward or Crystal of Clarity); degraded - a tool (attractor, enrichment staff or light orb), charged collectors, sometimes a pet summon and Arcana seeds.
- Chests are always unbreakable, after opening too: they do not break, explosions do not take them, movers do not move them. If a special pickaxe still breaks a chest, the chest itself does not drop.
- Appearance: the Arcana wall stone of its key, darker edges, a lock in a muted color of the key. Textures are built at launch from the installed DivineRPG, and they are not in the jar.
- Settings (the arcana section): arcanaChests, arcanaChestChance, arcanaChestPity, arcanaChestRegion.
