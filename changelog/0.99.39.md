## New lairs: an underground city and an ice dome

### Snow Glacon ice dome
- From the igloo a shaft with a spiral staircase leads down, and a tunnel into the ice dome.
- In the center is a snow nest with an altar on a pedestal, around it a frozen lake and a snowy shore, ice stalactites hang from the vault, in places growing together with the floor.
- The boss arena is the nest and the lake (radius 32).

### Ancient Dramcryx underground city
- Under the jungle temple is an underground city in a dome: the temple well turns into a tower with a spiral staircase to the floor.
- Streets with lanterns, overgrown houses with collapsed walls, ruins, gardens, chests in roughly every third house, roots hang from the vault.
- In the east is a square-arena with columns and an altar.

### General
- Light in the domes is sparse, for looks: foreign hostile mobs do not spawn inside, and the vault is no longer lined with lamps.
- Fluids from springs do not flow inside, and vanilla dungeons and mineshafts are not placed in the city area.
- Affects new chunks: already built lairs stay as they were.
