## Divine armor - a tier for Eden

- A full set of Divine armor is tier R1: the Great Eden Cadillion no longer hits it x3.
- Elite Realmite is now tier R0 (the cadillion hits it x3) and is still the entry to Wanderer armor: Realmite + Elite Realmite on the anvil = Wanderer R1.
- In tooltips tier 1 is called "Divine armor".
