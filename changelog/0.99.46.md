## Easter eggs: refinement

- The crowbar is a single model without texture flicker, thinner, with a tighter bend and a wide flat claw.
- The lambda on the suit chest is a round orange badge, as in Half-Life.
- Coinciding faces of the suit model (boots, knees, glasses) were removed - they no longer flicker.
- The suit voice, the dash sound and the whiff sound play for the player, not at the place where they occurred.
- H.E.V. armor icons are straight from the front, like regular armor.
