## Twilight dimension weather and sea weapons

### Eden: overheating
- The Eden sun never sets. Under the open sky the overheating bar fills in 5 minutes, drops in shade, and drops faster in water. At 50 weakness, at 75 nausea, at 100 you burn.
- The Frozen set does not overheat. Inferno, Netherite, the Eden set, fire protection and the cold perk on Wanderer armor, and Wanderer armor from R2 - twice as slowly.

### Wildwood: downpour
- Once every 15-25 minutes for 2-3 minutes. Under the open sky Slowness I, and mobs heal gradually.
- The Wildwood set heals in the downpour, as in water. Kraken, Aquastrive, water perks and Wanderer armor from R3 do not get wet.
- Sea weapons in water and in rain hit 25% harder, in a downpour so does water armor; together only once.
- Wildwood crafts: armor needs whale fins, a blade needs a shark anchor, a bow, phaser and blitz need liopleurodon teeth, and tools need a crab claw. Items that are already crafted do not change.

### Apalachia: rockfall
- Once every 10-15 minutes for a minute. A marker is visible on the ground before the impact. Boulders do not break blocks.
- A roof protects, and the Apalachia set takes no damage.
- Apalachia golems are not knocked back, and mauls and maces hit them 1.5 times harder.

### Anchors: harpoon
- A thrown anchor pulls the target toward the player, with a 3 second cooldown. The next hit with this anchor in hand within 2 seconds is 1.5 times stronger.
- Range: crab 8, shark 12, Greenland 12 with a one-second stun, liopleurodon 16 and up to three targets. From the water one and a half times farther.
- The harpoon does not move a boss, but pulls the player toward it. Players are pulled only with melee.pvp and not in a protected region.
