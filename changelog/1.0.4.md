## Added

- Scorcher Brand: crafted from Fury Fire, 4 blaze rods and 4 magma cream. Right-click into the air summons a small scorcher that runs alongside, fights in melee only and sets every target it hits on fire for 4 seconds. It does not burn itself. 6 arcane damage, 10 Arcana per second, attacks enemies within 8 blocks.
- Ayeraco Prism: crafted from the six Ender swords (purple, blue, green, yellow, red, gray) and two Eyes of Ender around an Ayeraco Scale. Right-click into the air summons a small Ayeraco that keeps shifting through the horde colors and flies alongside. When its target (or whoever attacks you) is more than 3 blocks away, it teleports right next to it, at most once every 2 seconds. 7 arcane damage, 10 Arcana per second, attacks enemies within 14 blocks.
- Ayeraco Scale: every Ayeraco killed by a player drops one.

## Changed

- The eye of the Cyclops Wand has its own model now: a veined eye with a red iris, an optic nerve tail and two flapping leathery stubs, instead of a small yellow Watcher.
- Summoning rules are shared with the other summons: one kind at a time, the oldest leaves when the cap is reached, upkeep is paid in Arcana, they only take damage from other players where PvP is allowed.
