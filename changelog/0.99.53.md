## King of Scorchers: a new fight

- Fixed: the King of Scorchers' fire shots and meteor rain did not work in the game (like the Watcher fight before), and he was just a big melee mob. Now they work everywhere.
- In his chamber he is a racer and runs laps on the track. A fire trail stretches behind him: standing in it means fire and ignition. If he hits a player - a ram with knockback and ignition. While running he shoots fire, every 12 seconds he calls meteors, and from time to time releases Scorchers onto the track (up to 4, no loot, they die with him).
- After three laps - a pit stop at the checkered finish line: he stands for 4 seconds and does not attack, the flame shield is off - point-blank hits go through in full.
- Below 35% health he sometimes cuts across the walkways and the dais in the center - sitting it out there no longer works.
- What to hit with: the flame shield halves point-blank hits, but water weapons (water dagger, maul and trident) - water puts out flame - hit one and a half times harder, and at the pit stop two and a half. Maelstrom and Sound of Music - one and a half times.
- What to wear: a full Inferno or Netherite set. The set's fire resistance puts out his trail and ignition, and the ram, shots and meteors do half damage against such a set.
- The chamber has 8 posts instead of 6 (not on the axes, the king cuts across along the axes), the railings were removed from the walkways.
- Tooltips of water weapons and of Inferno and Netherite armor, and Vatticus' advice about the king, were updated.
