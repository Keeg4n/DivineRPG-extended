## H.E.V.: own sounds for everyone

- The H.E.V. suit sounds are now available to everyone, without Half-Life installed: own sounds made for the add-on - footsteps on surfaces (stone, dirt, gravel, sand, wood, metal, ladder, water), the monitor beep on death, a servo on dash, a swing, a crowbar hit on a block and on a mob, an RPG shot.
- The suit voice is its own, in Russian: when the full suit is put on "Defense complex activated. Welcome, operator...", as well as morphine, a minor and a major fracture, critical health. The suit beep goes before the phrase.
- Those who have Half-Life and Half-Life 2 installed still hear the sounds from the games; the own ones are a fallback. Pain and falling without the games use regular Minecraft sounds.
