## Vethea: the fourth floor and the Infusion Altar

### Infusion Altar
- You can put more boulders on the altar than needed - exactly as many as the recipe requires will be used.
- Dream Wings are made at the altar, not at a crafting table: Dream Wings Template + 8 Amthirmis boulders = Dreamer's Wings; Midnight Wings Template + 12 Cermile boulders (from the Hive Queen) = Midnight Wings; tempering with Karos and 16 Quadrotic boulders = Nightmare Wings.
- The Hunger sells the wings templates (for gray and blue pearls), and the Heiva Belt is now 30 red pearls from him.
- Lunar weapons: an Arksiane weapon + 4 Lunar Shards (from Lady Luna). Everlight hits harder than Karos' maul.
- NEI got an Infusion Altar page, and the templates have a tooltip about what they produce.

### Combat in Vethea
- Some Vethea mobs are weak to their own weapon type: x2 damage from it and x0.25 from others; a mob finished off with its type drops loot twice. The list is `vetheaWeakness` in the config.
- L Heiva can be hit without the Heiva Belt.
- The Heiva Belt and the Miner's Amulet are worn in accessory slots. The belt gives rhythm: a hit with a different weapon type +15% damage, up to +60%, 3 seconds without hits - reset.
- Full Vethea set: the helmet boosts melee, the mask - ranged, the hood - arcana; any full set absorbs part of explosion damage. In Vethea explosions do not destroy items lying around.
- The Hive Queen is immortal: her bar shows how much of the swarm is left to kill; swarm killed - the Queen dies.
- Lady Luna fights in three phases: on the ground only melee hurts her, in the sky - bow and staff (the staff aims on its own), during lunar explosions - only the cannon.

### The dream is conquered
- Until all Vethea bosses are defeated, dream weapons do not hurt in the waking world and dream armor does not protect; after the victory the nightmare in Vethea is lifted for the player.
- The last bosses are counted in turn and unlock Wanderer perks: Regeneration, Arcana Flow, Angelic Flight.
