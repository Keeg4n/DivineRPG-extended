## The Watcher: a new fight

- Fixed the main issue: the add-on's Watcher fight did not turn on in the game, and the regular Watcher from Divine worked. Now the add-on fight works in the lair, and Divine's own flight and shot are disabled there.
- First phase. The Watcher hangs above the hall and charges a shot when it sees you. While it charges, the eye is open: projectiles hit it twice as hard. The Ghast Cannon disrupts the charge and stuns it.
- Hide behind a column and it releases Servant Eyes: small eyes fly to the cover and bite point-blank. No more than 5 at once, and they die together with it.
- At half health it roars (invulnerable at this time) and goes into the second phase. It releases a flock of eyes, then charges at you in dashes: wind-up (eye open), dash, after three dashes - a breather with one shot. Below 20% there are four dashes and the wind-up is shorter.
- It crashes into a column or wall - stunned and overheated: double damage to it. The Ghast Cannon during a wind-up also disrupts a dash.
- Explosions in the lair no longer break blocks and do not touch items on the ground. Explosion damage to the player is no more than 14 before armor.
- A full Bedrock or Ender set protects from explosions only after defeating the Watcher. The "Explosion Protection" perk is unlocked for it anyway.

## The Watcher's lair: the Eye observatory

- The hall under the lava lake was redone. On the floor is a mosaic of an eye (the white from quartz, the iris from red clay, an obsidian pupil) and rays to the walls. Eight obsidian columns with quartz bases and lamps are cover. In the walls are 11 red glass windows with lava behind them. In the center of the vault is a glass oculus through which lava can be seen. On the vault are glowstone stars.
- Entrance: the tunnel is wider and taller, with Nether brick ribs and glowstone, at the hall a portal with quartz columns. At the top is a gatehouse tower with obsidian turrets and an eye above the gate and a pier on piles. A courtyard is dug around the gate so the entrance is visible even if it is in rock.
- Lava and blood lakes from Nether generation (including Biomes O' Plenty) no longer carve out the lair's floor and walls: what they spoiled, generation restores from the blueprint.
- The King of Scorchers' Fire Chamber can no longer land on the Watcher's lair (before, it occasionally overlapped the entrance). Only such overlapping chambers are shifted.
- There is no rubble anymore. A lair built with rubble rebuilds itself to the new look when a player enters and the Watcher is not there (or on summoning).

## Fixes

- The server could crash when creating a new world if a city under the jungle temple, a Glacon dome or a tomb under a pyramid was generated near the spawn point.

## Nether ranged weapons

- Ghast Cannon: a heavy shot. Disrupts the Watcher's charge and dash, and disrupts the fire breath and attack run of the Ayeraco horde dragon.
- Maelstrom: the projectile pierces up to 3 targets and passes through the blue Ayeraco's shield.
- Reloading at a crafting table: the Ghast Cannon with Ghast tears, Maelstrom with purple clumps, +50 shots each. Available after defeating the King of Scorchers.
- Sound of Music and Sound of Carols: no more often than once per 8 ticks, the sound no longer overlaps, an extra click does not spend a charge. Notes fly without dropping and bounce twice off walls. Music hits boss minions for double. Carols slows the target, and hits the Frost Colossus and Snow Glacon one and a half times harder.
- The King of Scorchers in a flame shield: point-blank weapon hits - by half, Maelstrom and Sound of Music - one and a half times.
- Each weapon's role is written in its tooltip.
