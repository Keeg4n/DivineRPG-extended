## Corrupt altar: books for rupees

- Book enchantments are revealed for rupee ingots instead of arcana ore: 1 for the first slot, 3 for the second, 5 for the last. If the config still has arcana ore or the 1 / 5 / 10 price from 0.99.71, they are replaced automatically.

## Auto-smelting without Fortune for rupee and arlemite

- The Tinkers' Construct "Lava" modifier smelts mined ore by itself, and with Fortune on the tool it multiplied the ingots (up to 4 with Fortune III). For rupee and arlemite ore this no longer works: the block always gives one smelt, exactly like a furnace. Other ores are untouched.
- The ore list is the smeltNoFortune setting (modid:name, one per line; an empty list - Fortune works everywhere).

## Selftests

- Altar window test: a book - rupee ingots 1 / 3 / 5.
- New "Auto-smelting" test: a simulation (rupee and arlemite ore broken by a player - 4 ingots at the block center turn into 1; iron and ingots without breaking are untouched) and, if Tinkers' Construct is present, real mining with a pickaxe with Lava and Fortune III: rupee and arlemite always give 1 ingot each, iron gives more.
