## Cases from the tester's sheet

### Boss altars
- A broken altar (some pickaxes from modpacks break even the unbreakable) drops nothing. The altar item does not fall to the ground and disappears from the inventory in survival.
- In creative mode, an empty hand on the Mortum arena altar summons its boss without a crystal. Creative mode already summoned the Twilight dimension guardians on the nest altars without a Call.

### Ayeraco aerie
- There is a horde altar in the center of the arena: the Horde Horn sounds only on it, and in creative mode with an empty hand too. In aeries that are already built, the altar appears by itself when a player is nearby.
- Nothing spawns on its own within 160 blocks of the island center: no endermen, no Divine or other mod mobs.

### Mortum
- The Bone Pit and other arenas no longer hang over a cave: the floor layer under the walls and stands is filled in. In arenas that are already built, these voids are filled in by themselves when a player approaches.
- The Lethe river no longer keeps holes: what was scooped out with a bucket and the spot of a broken block are flooded again.
- Lethe eats arcana (25 per second) and does not let it regenerate. It bites (3 damage per second) only when there is no arcana left.

### NEI
- The Wanderer's Anvil in NEI, with slots as in the anvil itself: R1 forging from each final set with the fee and R2-R7 tempering with a world set and stones. All Wanderer armor is visible in NEI and in creative mode: R1 with each core and R2-R7.
- The tooltip at the bottom of the infusion altar was removed.
- Add-on items in NEI follow the creative mode tabs, and by name inside them (its own sorting step right after "by mods", visible in the NEI settings).

### Translation
- Russian terminology: End instead of Ender, bedrock instead of base rock, Halite instead of Galite, Corrupted armor instead of corruption.
- Divine descriptions written directly in the code in English were translated, and the missing Divine strings too. The names of donor sets are now in Russian.

### Structures
- Wanderer camps, ruins and ponds in the Twilight dimensions no longer stand on a dirt pedestal and no longer cut the slope out with a box: the platform is at the average height of the plot, with a smooth slope to the terrain around it.
