## Added

- Bows with a fast draw (Icicle, Wildwood, Skythern, Halite, Twilight, Shadow) shoot by themselves as soon as the string is fully drawn, and immediately draw again while right-click is held.
- The /drerank command: Divine ranks (0-3) of all players online and which boss opens the next one. On a rank increase - a chat message.
- H.E.V. suit: backwards bunnyhop. Back + Shift + jump sets the speed, every next jump right after landing speeds up (up to a limit), and the direction follows the look. A wall, water, a ladder or released keys reset the acceleration.

## Changed

- Endless Quiver from archers: a 0.4% chance, +0.2% per Looting level, no more than 1% (before, 10% +2%). From the Eternal Archer - as before, always.

## Other

- Only the selftest of these changes was run; bunnyhop is computed on the game side and is not checked by the server selftest.
