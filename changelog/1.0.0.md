DivineRPG Extended 1.0.0 - the first full release. An add-on for DivineRPG 1.4.2.3 (1.4.1.5 is supported too) for Minecraft 1.7.10 / Forge 10.13.4.1614 that turns DivineRPG into one connected RPG progression.

## Highlights

- **Boss progression.** New early-game bosses with their own lairs: Carcinus, Snow Glacon, Frost Colossus and the Lich King. DivineRPG mobs start spawning as you defeat bosses (spawn ranks 0-3, see `/drerank`). Arcana opens only after The Watcher, every Twilight dimension after the guardian of the previous one, Vethea after the Spirit of the Universe bosses.
- **Wanderer armor.** One armor set for the whole game: forged from a final DivineRPG set into R1, tempered by every dimension up to R7 at the Wanderer's Anvil, and crowned at R8. DivineRPG armor sets become perk donors; accessories go into armor sockets.
- **Iceika.** Cold and blizzards, the Frozen Throne, Rime Ore, frostbite weapons.
- **Twilight dimensions and Vethea.** Weather events (Eden sun, Wildwood downpour, Apalachia rockfall), mini-bosses, Vethea floors and dream rules, reliquary items.
- **Weapons with roles.** Melee kinds with their own timing, ranged roles, loading ammo into weapons (sneak + right click), self-firing fast-draw bows, arcane damage and the arcana shield.
- **Terraria-style fishing.** 12 fishing rods, baits, zones, crates, Leorna's quests and relics.
- **Merchants.** Arcana merchants arrive as you progress and can be called with the Memory Crystal (Captain Merik, the War General, Lord Vatticus, Datticon, Zelus); Jack o'Man and the Livestock Merchant trade at night.
- **Quality of life.** Training dummy that measures damage exactly as on a player, NEI integration, a creative tab, an audit log for server admins, protected-region (WorldGuard pvp flag) aware weapon effects.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.7.10, Forge 10.13.4.1614.
- DivineRPG 1.4.2.3 (or 1.4.1.5) in `mods`. In the DivineRPG config set `Vethea Inventory = false`.
- NEI is optional.
