## Ayeraco horde balance

- Healing is weaker: the pillar - 0.4% health per second (was 1%), green - 1 (was 2). An Ayeraco hit by a player does not heal for 5 seconds (10 in green End armor).
- While an Ayeraco breathes flame, damage to it is x1.5.
- Damage to the player is lower: breath 2 per 5 ticks (was 4), projectile up to 5 (was 7), burning ground less often.
- The flame is purple (void): instead of ignition - void burning, put out by water and Fire Resistance.
- During the fight on the island nothing spawns on its own, and outside hostile mobs disappear. Every 20 seconds the horde calls for reinforcements: Ender Triplets or an End Watcher, no more than three; after the fight the reinforcements disappear.
- Ender Bow: x2 against Ayeraco, arrows pass through the blue one's shield and put a void anchor on for 3 seconds - the Ayeraco is pulled down, cannot fly or attack. A void step toward an anchored Ayeraco carries you right up to it.
- The void step works with any End weapon: End swords of all colors, the Ender Bow, the Enderice.
