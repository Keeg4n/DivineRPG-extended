## Soft landing as a perk

- Wanderer armor no longer negates fall damage by itself: that was a legacy of the time when the first tier was forged from elite realmite.
- Instead there is a new perk "Soft landing": it is applied in the Wanderer's Anvil, the donor is a full elite realmite set, the fuel is arlemite, as with other perks. No boss is needed.
- Sets that are already forged lose fall protection; to get it back, apply the perk (a free slot is needed).
