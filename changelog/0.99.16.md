## Lakes in the Vethea ceiling

- Hanging lakes on stalactites were replaced by lakes in the ceiling: a bowl is carved into the underside of the slab above the floor, with the Dream Water surface flush with the ceiling. Affects new chunks; already generated stalactites remain.
- Swim up from below and you are pulled to the surface; as soon as your feet go into the water, the player hangs upside down: feet in the water, head in the air, and can breathe. The picture is flipped, and from the outside the player also appears upside down.
- This is how you fish: cast the rod into the lake, the bobber stays at the surface, and the catch goes straight into the inventory.
- Shift - dive deeper, jump at the surface - let go and fall.
- The server no longer kicks a player hanging at the surface for flying.
