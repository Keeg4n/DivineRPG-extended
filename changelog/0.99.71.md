## Corrupt altar: price by what was rolled

- Enchantments are no longer frozen: they are rolled anew every time you put an item into the altar. Take it out and put it back - new enchantments, and what you paid to reveal is lost. While the item is in the altar, what is revealed is exactly what will be applied to the item.
- The last slot no longer guarantees the best enchantments. The slot sets only the roll power (enchantment levels and how many there will be), and which enchantment drops follows the usual weights. Sharpness V in the last slot of a diamond sword drops in about half of the cases, Protection IV on a chestplate a bit less often.
- The price depends on the rolled enchantments. Each enchantment at full level adds to the price:
  - best (Protection, Sharpness, Power, Efficiency, Fortune, Looting, Silk Touch, Infinity): item +35, book +45;
  - medium (Unbreaking, Feather Falling, Fire Aspect, Flame, Respiration, Aqua Affinity, Thorns, Luck of the Sea, Lure, enchantments from other mods): item +12, book +15;
  - weak (Smite, Bane of Arthropods, Punch, Knockback, Fire Protection, Blast Protection, Projectile Protection): item +6, book +8.
  A partial level counts proportionally (Fortune I - a third of Fortune III). An item costs no more than 50 levels. A book costs 30 levels plus the enchantments and no more than 120. Examples: a Fortune III book - 75, Fortune III + Unbreaking III - 90, Efficiency V + Fortune III + Unbreaking III - 120; a sword with Sharpness V + Unbreaking III - 47.
- Within one group the price is the same on purpose: the price shows how good the roll is, and which exact enchantment it is only shows up after payment.
- Experience is deducted as if levels were counted from zero: enchanting for 50 levels always costs 3395 experience, a book for 120 costs 34440 (about 3 Karos), no matter how many levels you have. Before, 50 levels from level 150 cost 36175 experience. The tooltip above the slot shows how much experience will go (setting altarXpFromZero; false - take levels from the top, as usual).
- What you reveal enchantments with depends on the item: swords - Molten shards (a Molten stone for the last slot), pickaxes, axes, shovels and fishing rods - Terran, armor - Jungle, bows - Ender, everything else - corruption as before. Books - arcana ore: 1 for the first slot, 5 for the second, 10 for the last. Hover over the empty payment cell to see what to pay with for this item.
- In creative mode, enchanting no longer deducts levels.
- New settings: altarBookBase, altarBookMax, altarItemPoints, altarBookPoints, altarXpFromZero, altarBookRevealCost, altarRevealItems (what to pay with for each kind of item, by item name). altarTopLevels is now the cap of the item price.

## Selftests

- The "Corrupt altar" tests were rewritten: the prices from the examples above, experience up to levels 30/50/120, 1000 rolls of a sword, a chestplate and a book (prices within limits, the average price grows from slot to slot, the best enchantments in the last slot are neither always nor never present, books fit one item); the window (payment by item kind, other shards are not accepted, ore for a book, moving the item gives a new roll, experience deducted from zero, exactly the rolled enchantments, returning items, creative mode, shift-click); on opening, the old altar seed from 0.99.70 is erased.
