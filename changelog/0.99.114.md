## Wanderer armor R8: Crown

- A new tier R8 for completing Vethea. Tempering in the Wanderer's Anvil: an R7 set on top, a property set in the donor slot, 1 Spirit of the Universe as fuel. You must defeat Lady Luna.
- The Crown is one property of your choice, set by the donor set:
  - Angelic - flight as in creative mode, without limits (except in Vethea itself);
  - Vemos - health regeneration;
  - Korma - arcana flow: arcana regenerates faster, Arcana sword hits do not stop regeneration, arcane damage x2 (the bonus of Arcana swords and staff projectiles; the regular part of a sword hit does not grow).
- Changing the Crown on R8: an R8 set on top, a set of another property in the donor slot, 8 arlemite as fuel.
- Flight, regeneration and arcana flow can no longer be applied as minor perks. If they were already on R1-R7 armor, they stop working and their slots are freed.
- R8: full set protection 85%, 4 minor slots (the tierFullReduction and tierSmallSlots settings are extended with a value for R8).
- Each Crown has its own R8 look:
  - flight - Wraith: pale blue plate armor, tattered ghostly ribbons and wisps behind the back. A deep hood with an arched cutout and a glowing edge, a smoothed tunnel inside, darkness in the depth and two blue eyes. The hood has no tail. The plate armor is translucent, the darkness under the hood is not;
  - regeneration - Lunar: silver with lilac, a crescent above the helmet and on the chest, a sickle moon on the back;
  - arcana - Dreamer: indigo with stars and a golden thread, a third eye, a dream catcher on the back.
- Accessories on the body are fitted to all three R8 looks as well. Piece icons for all three.
- Settings (balance): upgradeR8Spirits - how many Spirits of the Universe the tempering costs, crownArcanaDamage - the arcane damage multiplier of the arcana Crown.
- NEI: R7 to R8 tempering pages for each Crown (an R7 set, a property set in the donor slot, a Spirit of the Universe, Lady Luna).
- Hotfix: released without a full run of the selftests. The new R8 checks and the related checks of armor, anvil, accessories and Arcana passed (19 of 19); not checked in game, and the Wraith translucency is visible only in the client.
