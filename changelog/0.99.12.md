## Fixes

- Lair maps no longer freeze the game during a long-range search: the search runs in parts, first the message "The map is unfolding" arrives, and a few seconds later the answer.
- Fixed a crash when loading ice cave chunks in a modpack with the Hats mod.
- Divine bosses now have boss status, like the Wither and the dragon: mods that spare bosses now spare them too (including the Blood Magic sacrificial dagger).
- Lethe Bucket: an empty bucket used on a Lethe source gives a Lethe Bucket (before, it gave water), which places Lethe back. It also works with machines that understand fluid buckets.
- Fishing in lava in creative mode: the catch drops at your feet instead of disappearing.
