## Easter eggs: armor stand and a switch

- The H.E.V. suit is now visible on a BiblioCraft armor stand and on mobs in armor (before, regular Realmite armor was shown there).
- The `easterEggs` config setting: `false` turns off the easter eggs (suit, crowbar, RPG-7, the sofa in new chunks). All players receive the server's decision on joining.
