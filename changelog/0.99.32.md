## End armor void step

- The void step is a double W with an Ender sword in hand, from a standstill or right while running (the sprint key does not trigger the step). Speed is kept, and a step while running continues the run.
- The step lifts onto ledges up to 2 blocks high, and looking slightly down does not cut it off.
- The cooldown is shorter - you can step again and again.
- If the step fails (a wall ahead, an abyss under the destination, not enough arcana), the reason is written above the hotbar.
