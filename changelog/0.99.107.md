## Preparing for CurseForge: Divine textures are no longer in the jar

- The DivineRPG license does not allow moving its resources to other projects, and 32 add-on textures were recolors or reworks of Divine textures (halite tools, mage staffs, rails, the Watcher's eye, angler armor, the Ancient Dramcryx stripe, the turquoise tormented armor and others; three were exact copies). Now they are not in the jar: the add-on builds them at client start from the textures of the installed DivineRPG using its own color tables. In game everything looks as before. If a player has a different DivineRPG version with different textures, the recolor lands on its textures.
- On DivineRPG 1.4.1.5 (the Magic modpack) some of these Divine textures are different (redrawn at 32x32 or changed): there the add-on recolors the texture of that version into its own palette by brightness. The icons are similar but not the same: halite tools, mage staffs, the Arcanite Pickaxe, the jungle gem, Karot and Soul Fiend trophies, the angler vest, jungle ore. For the multitool, the Crystal of Clarity and the Arcane Ward, where 1.4.1.5 has a different drawing or none, a similar texture of that version is used.
- The mod description (mcmod.info) is in English, with the address and author; the license states how the add-on handles Divine textures.
- English translation: 39 missing strings were added (item tooltips, the Soul Fiend name).
