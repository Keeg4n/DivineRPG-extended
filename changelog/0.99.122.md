## Weaker mauls

- Mauls (water, bedrock, crab, fury, corrupted, Terran, toothed) hit x1.2 instead of x2 against mobs and x1.2 instead of x1.5 against players. With x2 they hit harder than the Karos Maul, and the bedrock and toothed ones also had an area hit. The target's invulnerability window (1 s) and the 1 s cooldown stayed.
- The melee.maul.damage setting (and damagePvp): if the config had the old default value (2.0 and 1.5), it is rewritten to 1.2 automatically. An admin's own value is not touched.
- Hotfix: released without a full run of the selftests, the weapon subtype and toothed weapon checks passed; not checked in game.
