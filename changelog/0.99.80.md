## Arcana fountain hall

- The hall no longer appears under the map. Arcana floors are at heights 8, 16, 24 and 32, but the hall counted them from zero: every fourth hall hung in the void below the world, and the top floor had no halls at all. Now halls stand on all four floors.
- This applies to new chunks. Halls that have already appeared under the map in an existing world stay where they are.

## Selftests

- The fountain hall test now builds halls with real generation in new chunks and checks that there is not a single block below the bottom floor, and that a hall under the map cannot be built.
