## Training dummy and water

- The training dummy no longer reacts to water. Before, it floated up in water (the old Minecraft AI "jumps" in water), and when the water was removed it kept bouncing: the jump flag was not reset. Now there is no jump, the current does not push it, and it does not drown underwater (drowning damage would break the hit count).

## Divine mobs on the Xaero minimap

- Xaero's Minimap draws the head of the mob model on the radar, but it cannot find it on Divine and add-on models - the mob disappeared from the map. The add-on provides Xaero with a description of every Divine and add-on mob (173 mobs): instead of an icon, a colored dot is drawn by mob type (enemies are red), as Xaero does for unknown creatures.
- The training dummy has its own icon on the minimap - a straw face, not a player head.

## Arcanite Pickaxe

- The regular Arcanite Pickaxe costs 20 arcanium at the War General instead of 40 (still after all the Mortum bosses).
- The new Charged Arcanite Pickaxe mines 3x3 (sneaking - a single block), like the Nightmare Drill. After all 14 Divine bosses (Ancient Entity, the Watcher, King of Scorchers, six Mortum bosses, Dramix, Parasecta, Raglok, Wreck, Lady Luna) the General exchanges the regular pickaxe and 20 arcanium for the charged one.

## Selftests

- New tests: the training dummy does not float or lose air in water for 4 seconds and does not bounce without water; every Divine and add-on mob in the game registry has a description for Xaero; the Charged Arcanite Pickaxe - prices and exchange at the General, a 3x3 wall of Arcana brick (9 blocks with the charged one, 1 when sneaking, 1 with the regular one).
