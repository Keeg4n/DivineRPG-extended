## End armor

- The void step no longer puts you into blocks: before, in a corner of blocks the center of the player ended up in air while the edges of the hitbox were in walls, and the player suffocated. Now the arrival point is checked against the whole hitbox, the step stops before a wall, and if there is nowhere to stand the step fails.

## Fire Chamber and the Watcher's lair

- Lava windows are closed with glass on all sides: on the diagonals of the round hall the lava behind a window could border air and flow into the hall. Affects new chunks; in already built chambers the windows patch themselves when a player goes inside or summons the boss (exposed lava becomes obsidian, lava that flowed into the hall is removed).
- Boss minions do not hurt their own: a stray Scorcher shot at a Scorcher (or a boss at its own minion) deals no damage, and they no longer start taking revenge on each other. Applies to the minions of all bosses.
