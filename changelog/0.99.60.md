## Easter egg

- Musou no Hitotachi was redrawn after the in-game sample: a very long thin blade with a smooth curve, glowing lilac, the edge almost white, along the blade a light stripe and a wave, at the base an engraved lightning bolt. Instead of a round golden tsuba there is a glowing swirl emblem (three tomoe commas) in a dark angular setting with spikes; the handle is dark, angular, with light inlays, the pommel cut at an angle; the tassel is gone.
- The blade and emblem glow: they are drawn at full brightness and without shadows, visible in the dark too.
- In hand the katana is 1.2 times longer than its already long model (the handle is still in the palm); the inventory icon was redrawn from the new model.
