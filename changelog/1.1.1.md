## Changed
- The Vethea mini-boss Sleepwalker appears only on floor 3 (floors 1-2 are too early for it) and always has the floor 3 strength (400 health).
- The Sleepwalker drops 4-6 Polished Pearls and 1-2 Shiny Pearls (Looting - a chance of one more Shiny) instead of the floor boulders. Finished off with its own weapon type, it drops twice as much, as before.
- The pause between mini-bosses (miniBossMinutes, 20 minutes by default) is shared by players near each other: two players together no longer get a mini-boss each a few minutes apart.
- Russian: the remaining "Король скорчеров" lines (cannon reload hints, the chamber map, the soul spark, the General order, the lore line) are "Король лихачей" now.
