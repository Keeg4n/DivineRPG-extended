## Changed
- Wanderer armor: more detailed textures for all looks. Metal (gold, steel, white and dark plate) has a light top edge, a cool shadow at the bottom, seams between the plates, rivets and scratches; cloth and leather have folds; wood has grain and knots; the bedrock stone has coarse grain and cracks; bright inlays and crystals have a glint. Shapes and colors are the same, glowing parts are unchanged, and dyed armor keeps the new shading.
## New
- Optional resource pack `DivineRPG-Extended-HD-Armor-1.1.13.zip`, attached to this release: the Wanderer armor in double resolution (4 texels per model pixel instead of 2), so the seams, rivets, scratches and the cloth weave are finer. Put the zip into `resourcepacks` and switch it on in the game. The pack brings its own armor models along with the textures, so use the pack of the same version as the addon.
## Fixed
- Models read from resources (Wanderer armor, the Wanderer Anvil, Easter eggs) are read again when resource packs change: a pack with its own models takes effect without restarting the game.
