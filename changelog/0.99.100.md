## Wanderer's Anvil: core change

- A Wanderer R1 with the needed core is now also suitable as a donor for changing the core: the core passes to the upgraded set, the donor is consumed, and no fuel is needed (the core was already paid for when this R1 was forged). Only an R1 works as a donor, an upgraded set is not consumed; an R1 bound to someone else is not accepted as a donor.
- A final set in the donor slot without fuel now asks for stones for changing the core, not arlemite for a perk, and hints that with arlemite the same set applies its own perk.
- If the core is already that one, the anvil says so (and reminds you about arlemite for a perk) instead of asking for fuel.
- The look changes only for R1: from R2 it is shared by all cores, and the core property stays.

## Ranged weapons

- Blitz uses 5 dust per shot (a dust per pellet). With less than 5 in the charge and the inventory, it does not fire; the tooltip has a "Per shot" line. The Dust Pouch and Infinity still remove the cost. The number is configurable (balance, blitzDustPerShot).
- On the icon of a bow, blitz, phaser, cannon and other ranged weapons in your own inventory and on the hotbar there is a number for the ammo supply (charge + inventory). Thousands show as 1k, a quiver or pouch shows an infinity sign, and less than one shot shows in red. There is no number in NEI and in chests. It can be disabled in the settings (client, ammoCountOnIcon).

## World and armor

- Divine Bedrock armor no longer wears out, and the tooltip shows infinite durability.
- Tinkers' Construct slime islands no longer appear in Divine worlds (in new chunks; ones that have already appeared stay).
- Twilight Demon cliff: the rule from 0.99.96 "fill in the floor layer under the walls" fired for it by mistake, although its platform is 12 blocks higher than the other arenas. In the narrow ring at the foot (40-44 blocks from the center) it put stone over the void, and the touch-up of old arenas could fill in up to 6 blocks there, including Lethe. Now the cliff is not touched by this rule. What was already filled in on worlds where the demon arena was visited on 0.99.96-0.99.99 is not removed by itself.
