## Early game: Overworld weapons have their own roles

Before, the first half of the game was played with ranged combat alone. Now almost every Overworld item has its own role.

## Summoner: slime - crab - eye - shark - wraith

- Slime Sword: right-click (into the air) summons a slime. The slime jumps nearby and hits the owner's target (3 arcane damage x stage). Costs 1 arcana per second, no more than 2 at once. The sword's melee is as before.
- Cyclops Wand: no longer shoots. Right-click summons an eye. The eye hangs as a companion overhead, flies through blocks and hits weaker than all summons (2 x stage), but each hit knocks the enemy away from the owner. It knocks back bosses less, and those resistant to knockback by their resistance. Costs 1 arcana per second, no more than 2. Cyclops eye shards are no longer needed for the wand.
- Each summon has its own aggro radius (blocks from the owner): slime 6, crab 8, eye 10, shark 12, wraith 16. Within this radius the summon attacks the nearest visible monster by itself, even if the owner hits no one. The summon does not chase the owner's target beyond its own radius. It does not touch zombie pigmen and endermen until they are angry; pets, players, the training dummy and other summons - never.
- The tooltip of each summon (under Shift) shows the hit damage and the aggro radius.

## Weapons

- Corrupted Cannon: in the Overworld the deeper you stand, the stronger it is. From height 64 and above x1, toward bedrock (height 5) it grows up to x1.6. In other worlds x1. The tooltip shows what the multiplier is right here.
- Golden Fury (shoots gold nuggets): mobs killed by it drop a gold nugget (25%) and a shadow coin (10%). With Infinity the nuggets are not needed: it makes a farm of gold and coins. Nothing drops from boss minions and mobs not of your stage.
- Stone-Blood Sword: vampirism. Heals for 8% of the damage dealt, and below a third of health - for 20%.
- Poison Saber: a mob it hit does not heal at all for 5 seconds (neither by regeneration, nor food, nor potions); the poison also remains. Nothing else has such an effect.
- Palavence, Massivence, Frossivence: left-click on a player or your own pet heals them instead of hitting (2 / 3 / 4 health, no more than once per 0.5 seconds per target, 1 durability per heal). It works in protected regions without PvP too: the hit is cancelled before damage, and the protection does not see it. Other people's pets and mobs are hit as usual. Right-click heals yourself, as before.
- Hunting Bow: sniper. Beyond 16 blocks the damage grows, from 32 blocks - x1.5.
- Shadow Bow: momentum. After 3 seconds of running, the next shot is stronger, up to +50%. The bow does not slow you while running: you draw it and keep running.
- Shuriken: streak. Each consecutive hit (no more than 2 seconds apart) gives +15% to the next one, up to +60%.
- Sneaky Hurricane: a poison cloud remains at the hit spot for 4 seconds, and it poisons mobs around. It does not touch players, pets and peaceful mobs.

## Throwables stack up to 256

- Shurikens, snow shurikens, Sneaky Hurricane and Twilight dimension slicers are collected into a single item of up to 256 pieces. In 1.7.10 a regular stack never exceeds 64.
- Sneak + right-click collects all the same ones from the inventory into the item in hand. The number is on the icon, and a throw uses one piece. Ones picked up from the ground add themselves to the collected stack. A collected stack does not merge with other stacks.
- The Thrower's Sling returns a slicer into the collected stack too.

## Serenades

- Serenade of Death: now a life charm. It lies in the inventory and saves from a lethal hit: full health and Regeneration II for 10 seconds, and the serenade itself breaks. A ready Pharaoh's Ankh saves first (it recharges, the serenade does not). It does not save in Vethea, from the void and from /kill. Right-click heals to full (15 uses). Recipe: a Serenade of Health (any wear) in the center and 4 Mortum stones in a cross. The old Divine recipe was removed.
- Serenade of Infusion: besides the Divine healing, also +50 arcana and Clarity for 10 seconds (arcana regenerates without a pause).
- Serenade of Health did not change.

## Anvil: enchantments for Divine ranged weapons

- Infinity, Unbreaking or Thaumcraft Repair can be applied to any Divine ranged weapon (bows, cannons, staffs, phasers, blitzes, La Vekor, Corrupted Cannon) with a book on an anvil. The enchantments that the anvil applies itself, it applies; Repair and Unbreaking are incompatible, as in Thaumcraft.
- Infinity on any ranged weapon (and on a regular bow): ammo is not needed and is not spent, and the icon shows an infinity sign. A bow with Infinity no longer needs an arrow in the inventory.
- Thaumcraft Repair on a Divine ranged weapon repairs it (every 2 seconds from the hotbar, for a vis fee).
- Vethea items follow their own rules, without these enchantments. A throwable without enchantments is its own ammo.

## Other

- Xaero minimap: summons (slime, crab, eye, shark) and Liopleurodon have a colored dot, like the other add-on mobs (the crab, shark and Liopleurodon had none since 0.99.113).
