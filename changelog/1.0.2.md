## Fixed

- Summons (crabs, slimes, eyes, sharks and arcane wraiths) now defend their owner: whoever hits the owner - a mob, an archer by its arrow, or a player where PvP is allowed - becomes their target. Before, they reacted only to what the owner hit, and to mobs that announced the owner as their target, which many mobs never do.
