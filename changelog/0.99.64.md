## Easter egg

- Musou no Hitotachi: the guard was refined using in-game screenshots and a reference picture:
  - the dark plate under the tomoe is no longer a thick "mitten": it is lower and narrows toward the blade like a wedge, the blade comes out of it at full width (before - a thin neck from the end);
  - the guard spikes became noticeable: two dark spikes on top are taller and sharper, below is a long curved spike toward the handle (before the plate almost hid them);
  - the three tomoe commas are shifted slightly toward the center of the plate - they lie inside the dark frame, as on the model;
  - the purple flames on the blade start right from the guard.
