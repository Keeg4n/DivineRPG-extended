## Fixed

- Wanderer armor: faces that lay exactly on top of each other inside one look no longer flicker and show through each other. Thirteen looks had such faces (from 2 to 56 pairs); now there are none. The armor generator removes them by itself on every rebuild.
- Wanderer armor and the H.E.V. suit on players take the pose straight from the player: sneaking, arm swing, the drawn bow, blocking, riding. Before, the pose was copied from the player's main model, which other mods can leave in another state - then the armor stood straight while the player sneaked or swung. This was most visible on the translucent Wraith (Crown of Flight R8), where the player's body is hidden under the armor. Players and mobs now use separate armor model instances, so drawing armor on a mob or a stand cannot reset a player's pose.
