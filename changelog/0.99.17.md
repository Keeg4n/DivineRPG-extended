## Fixes and improvements

### World
- Fast flight over Vethea no longer drops TPS: Vethea generation decorations do not create neighboring chunks in a chain. Structures are built whole, as before.
- Frozen Throne without holes in the walls and vault: ice caves, lakes and Rollum dungeons do not appear next to it, and the Throne itself fills holes left by other mods' generators according to the blueprint. Affects new Thrones.
- Jungle pyramids and temples also appear in Biomes O' Plenty biomes; if there is no jungle near spawn, the temple spawns in warm green biomes (`scatterInModBiomes`). Affects new chunks.
- The ice cave is also placed in cold biomes without snow (taiga, mountains, BoP cold forests), snow is piled around the igloo and water freezes. Affects new chunks.
- A new portal in Eden, Wildwood, Apalachia and Skythern is placed on the nearest flat area of the island, not over the void.
- Twilight dimension biomes have their own temperature and humidity (Eden is hot and dry, Skythern is cool and humid, etc.).

### Bosses and mobs
- After the fourth totem the Wind Lord descends to the ground with 1 health, the wind dies down - he can be finished off with anything, and the victory is counted.
- A tamed Moon Wolf keeps the wild one's health (200); those already tamed will rise to 200 on their own.
- Lethe hurts mobs once per second, except undead and Mortum villagers.

### Items and blocks
- The Nether Torch recipe is like the regular one: coal above an ifrit rod.
- The Wanderer in the Apalachia camp sells a sanctum seal for 4 world stones.
- Carcinus burrow blocks can be broken again, as before; in the inventory they still become regular sandstone and sand.
- Apalachia Rails placed before 0.99.13 remember their shape and keep their turn and switch, like new ones.
- Dream Water flips the player immediately on touch, and the side view and F5 are also upside down. Space - dive deeper, Shift - let go and fall.
