## New icons chosen by the tester

- Twilight dimension mob materials: a fiery sun feather, a spiral Eden horn shard, a knobby greenfeet root with a sprout, a verek fang, a spiral moon horn in a ring, a rune cube of the moss core, an amethyst cube with a rune heart, a starry dusk horn, an Apalachian warrior medallion, a sky essence crystal, a sky string spool, a compressed cloud cube, a split dark horn with heat inside, a demon heart in dark flame, a soul silk web.
- Boss loot: faceted guardian hearts, time gears with a dial (before, these were Divine world stones), Mortum trophies (a black heart in flame, an eye in a crosshair, two halves of a circle, a ghost in a cage, a tusk with a golden ring, a faceted core), a spiky red Shell of the Deep (before, Divine water pellets), a frozen heart with a scarlet core, a Frozen Throne shard with a rune, the Watcher's eyeball, a lunar shard sickle. Soul sparks and the halite shard are as before.
- Wanderer's Clock: a pocket watch, each level with its own icon. The case changes from copper to black steel, and at each level the stone of the next world lights up.
- The new icons were drawn from scratch and live in the mod itself; Mortum trophies and the Eye of the Watcher are no longer built from Divine textures at launch.
