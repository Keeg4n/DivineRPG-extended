## Biomes O' Plenty compatibility

- Divine biome IDs matched Biomes O' Plenty biomes, and biomes got mixed up. Now at startup the occupied Divine IDs are moved to free ones automatically (starting from 170). In already generated chunks of Divine worlds, biomes may remain foreign.
- Lairs recognize biomes from other mods: the Carcinus lair spawns on BoP sandy beaches, the ice cave in BoP snowy areas, the lair under the jungle temple in BoP jungles. Affects new chunks.
- The Bestiary and fishing take BoP biomes into account: the Nether and beaches from other mods.
- Fixed a crash when saving a chunk with a lone Ayeraco (without pack siblings), and on killing it, in a modpack with Morph.
