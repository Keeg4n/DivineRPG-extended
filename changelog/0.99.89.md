## Bedrock steadfastness

Full Bedrock set and the Bedrock core in Wanderer armor: protection builds up while you stand still.
- After 2 seconds Resistance I (damage -20%) and no knockback, after 5 seconds Resistance II (damage -40%).
- You can attack, eat and look around. A step or a jump resets the stance, and the effect ends in half a second.
- Numbers in the config: bedrockStanceTicks.

## Nether armor smelts ore

Full Inferno or Netherite set: mined ore has a 25% chance to drop already smelted, one to one, as from a furnace.
- Does not work with Silk Touch. Does not affect slag, logs and sand, only ore.
- Chance in the config: netherSmeltChance.

## Goggles of Revealing in the helmet

Thaumcraft Goggles of Revealing can now be embedded into a helmet in the Wanderer's Anvil, "Sockets" tab. The helmet shows Thaumcraft nodes and hints and gives a 5% vis discount, like the goggles themselves.
- Divine and Wanderer helmets fit, and the goggles can be taken back out for free.
- The goggles do not take an accessory slot. A Divine helmet has a second socket for them, while a Wanderer helmet puts the goggles into the second socket right away, without waiting for Karot; a second accessory will not fit next to them.
- Without Thaumcraft nothing changes.
