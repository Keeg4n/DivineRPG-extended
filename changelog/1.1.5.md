## Changed
- No quartz in the addon's structures any more: the white stone is now Divine milk stone, a block that nothing else generates (it is only crafted from stone and a milk bucket). This covers the Mortum arenas (the light half and the sun of the Hall of Twins, the bones of the Bone Pit), the giant skulls and rib cages of Mortum, the Sun sanctuary of Eden and the Temple of Winds of Skythern, the Watcher lair (the eye white, the columns, the sills), the King of Scorchers pit and the Ayeraco nest. Carved and pillar quartz became plain milk stone as well; quartz slabs became smooth stone slabs (Divine has no white slab). New chunks only: structures that are already built keep their quartz.
## Fixed
- The Frost Colossus pauldron and the Ayeraco wing went to every player within 64 blocks, without the stage check: killing them too early still gave the accessory, and so did just standing nearby. Now, like the other boss accessories, they go only to those who hit the boss and have reached its stage: the Colossus - the Lich King's stage (after Carcinus), the Ayeraco horde - after the Ancient Entity; the wing goes to everyone who hit any dragon of the horde, when the last one falls.
- The Frost Colossus had no stage at all: killed too early, it still dropped its loot. Now it is the Lich King's stage, like the Lich King himself.
## Other
- Rebuilding an old Watcher lair still recognizes its quartz as the lair's own block.
- Selftests: the Temple of Winds spire, the giant remains and the Watcher lair look for milk stone.
