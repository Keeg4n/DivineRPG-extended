## Easter eggs: the new H.E.V. suit and Half-Life sounds

- The H.E.V. suit was redone entirely: a detailed model (chestplate, lambda badge, stripes, shoulder pads, bracers, thighs, shin guards, boots), and the order of armor parts was fixed.
- The helmet has two looks: the Mark IV helmet and Gordon's glasses - the helmet alone in a crafting table switches the look.
- The crowbar is a volumetric model with the claw bent forward; new icon.
- Sounds are taken directly from installed Half-Life and Half-Life 2 (Steam is found automatically; the path can be set with the `halfLifePath` setting). The conversion script is no longer needed.
- The crowbar sounds on every swing (at a mob, a block or air); running in the suit plays the suit sound from Half-Life 2; footsteps on surfaces come from Half-Life 2.
