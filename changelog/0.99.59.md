## Easter egg

- Musou no Hitotachi is now obtained from Arcana's Storm Sword: a Storm Sword thrown on the ground and struck by lightning becomes the Musou no Hitotachi katana. All Storm Sword properties are kept, lightning does not burn it. The Serenade Striker no longer transforms (an already transformed one becomes a regular serenade).
- The katana is held in hand like a sword and with the handle exactly in the palm (before it hung below the hand), and in hand it is 1.4 times longer than a regular sword.
