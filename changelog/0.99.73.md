## Corrupt altar: each slot has its own price cap

- The second slot could cost as much as the last one (50 levels for a pile of medium enchantments) while being revealed for just 5 shards. Now each slot has its own cap: item - 17 / 33 / 50 levels, book - 60 / 90 / 120. The price is still calculated from the rolled enchantments but does not rise above the cap of its slot.
