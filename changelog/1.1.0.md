## Fixed - dupes
- Divine furnaces with roles: the bonus counted any drop of the ore in the input slot as a smelt, taking it back out by hand too. 64 diamond ore put into a Demon furnace and taken back out a tick later gave up to 64 diamonds, again and again. Now only real smelts count - when the output grows by the recipe result.
- Vethea Infusion Altar: the result was refreshed only once a tick, and the cost was taken by the inputs of the moment of pickup. Two clicks in one tick (take the template, then the result) or a pipe pulling the result slot gave the result for free. Now the result follows the inputs at once, the cost is charged exactly when the result leaves its slot, and nothing can be put into the result slot.
- Pet Whistle: two hits on your pet in one tick gave two eggs - the second a plain Divine egg that hatched a new pet. Now a released pet gives one egg, and only with the pet inside.
- Iceika: every population of a neighboring chunk refilled an emptied dungeon chest with loot again, and an empty decorative chest carried to the edge of the explored land got village loot. Now each chest is filled once; chests placed by players are never filled.
- Bone Ring and Steady Hand returned an arrow on a quick tap of the bow that fired nothing. Now only a really shot arrow that was spent can come back (not Infinity, not the Endless Quiver).
- Silk Touch on a temporary cloud of the Cloud Staff gave a permanent cloud block; the boss's temporary ice and the trap floor did the same. Now they cannot be taken with Silk Touch (permanent clouds still can).
- Jack o'Man's nightly coin cap was reset by dying. Now it survives death.
- Jack o'Man trades with one player at a time: the list rebuilt for a second player changed under the first one's window.
- The Wanderer Anvil and the Corruption Altar: leaving the End with the window open lost the items in the window. Now they come back to the player. The window also closes when the player is in another world.
- The one-time return of items locked in Divine's separate Vethea inventory no longer keeps what did not fit in the player's data: it goes to the inventory at once, the rest drops at the player's feet.
## Fixed - regions (claims)
- Effects of addon weapons, armor and accessories on villagers, animals and pets are now checked by the region plugin itself, with a trial hit from the responsible player (the owner of a pet or summon, the shooter of a projectile). Before, creatures were checked only by the pvp flag, which is not set in an ordinary region: the Aquatooth bleeding, fire, poison and slowness reached villagers and animals in someone else's region although the hit itself was cancelled. A bleeding creature stops bleeding when it is in a region where the attacker may not hurt it.
- Checked by the region now (before - not at all): the frostbite of ice weapons, the Poison Saber's no-healing, the lifesteal of the Bloodgem Sword and of the Mortum set, the harpoon pull of the Shark and Bowhead anchors, the Divine Attractor (creatures and carts out of someone else's region), the Tomahawk and the Vethea discs (damage and stun), the slowness of the Sound of Carols notes.
- The Pocket Magnet and a trained Paratiku do not take items out of someone else's region and do not take someone else's boss loot share. The Paratiku now brings items to the owner's feet, and the usual pickup takes them with all its checks.
- Not in someone else's region: scooping Lethe and Dream Water with a bucket, planting Dream Carrots, washing dye off in a cauldron, lighting a lure torch or a brazier, setting a campfire respawn point.
- A new twilight portal on the other side is not built in someone else's region or in spawn protection: the point moves to the nearest place where the player may build (the way back to the Overworld lands by the same coordinates - often in someone's base).
- A broken crab burrow fills with sand only the passage by its walls, and nothing in someone else's region: before, the whole 7x6x7 box was filled, reaching into a neighboring region, and every breach gave hundreds of free sand.
- The Wind Lord's arena never clears built blocks (only air, leaves and grass), and a Wind Totem left without its Wind Lord (a command, a lag cleaner) disappears by itself instead of staying unbreakable forever.
- The lightning of the Skythern Cadillion and the Wind Lord no longer sets blocks on fire.
- The Eternal Archer brings down cover columns only inside its gallery, not any carved stone near the target.
- Placing blocks by addon items now also respects spawn protection and plugins that forbid building through setBuild(false). If a server cannot be asked (another core), placing and effects on players and peaceful creatures are refused rather than allowed; the log says so once.
- Trial events of these checks no longer trigger addon reactions (furnace modules dropping, torch wards removed, the Soul Fiend's graveyard).
- On a server without Bukkit, claim mods are asked with trial Forge place and break events.
