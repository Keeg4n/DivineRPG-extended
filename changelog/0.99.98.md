## World armor without the previous set

- Twilight dimension armor no longer needs to be crafted from the previous world's set. Instead of a piece of the previous set, the recipe contains the previous world's time gear: Wildwood armor - the Eden gear, Apalachia - the Wildwood gear, Skythern - the Apalachia gear, Mortum - the Skythern gear. Eden armor is made only from Eden pieces and material. The rest of the recipes are as before.
- The time gear is no longer consumed anywhere: after crafting it stays in the crafting table. One gear both upgrades the Wanderer's Clock and is needed for armor. Gears no longer stack.
- Weapons and tools are still crafted from an item of the previous world. Halite did not change.

## Wanderer armor is bound to its owner

- A piece of Wanderer armor binds to whoever puts it on first. The owner is shown in the tooltip.
- On anyone else the armor gives nothing: no protection, no perks, no core, no accessories in the sockets, no fall protection. For bosses a non-owner counts as having no armor and takes triple damage.
- Only the owner can remove the binding, in the Wanderer's Anvil: Wanderer pieces on top, donor and fuel empty, the "Unbind" button. The armor will then bind to whoever puts it on next.
- Sets that are already forged will bind to whoever first puts them on after the update.

## Dyeing Wanderer armor

- A piece of Wanderer armor is dyed in a crafting table with regular dyes, like leather armor: colors mix, including with the one already applied.
- The main material of the look is recolored (gold and steel of R1, planks of R3, plate of R5 and so on), while the trim, gems and glow keep their own colors. The piece icon is dyed the same way.
- This is only the look: the tier, core, perks, sockets and owner do not change. To wash it off, right-click a water cauldron, which uses a third of the water.
