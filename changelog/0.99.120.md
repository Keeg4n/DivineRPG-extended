## Summons

- Summons of one owner are of one kind only: call a crab and the slimes, eye, sharks and wraiths leave, and vice versa.
- Full Korma set and the arcana Crown R8 - one more of each summon (crabs, slimes, eye, sharks - 3; wraiths, as before with Korma, - 3).
- The owner's right-click on their summon removes it; the anchor in hand does not summon a new one then. Someone else's summon cannot be removed.
- The Pet Whistle now works with summons too: it calls them to you, and a hit with the whistle removes them.
- The arcana price was raised: arcana regenerates 20 per second, and summons cost 1-3 and were not felt. Now per second: slime and eye 3, crab 5, shark 7, wraith 8.
- Slimes and crabs push each other and do not stand inside each other; ones teleported to the owner stand in a ring.
- If you hit the training dummy, the anchor summons hit the training dummy too (before, only wraiths and pets did).

## Training dummy and the arcana Crown

- The training dummy did not count the arcane strike of Arcana swords: it is a separate hit on the next tick and landed on the player's hidden copy inside the dummy. Now it counts. This is why the Livicia Sword showed identical damage with and without the arcana Crown: the Crown doubles exactly the arcane part of the strike, and on mobs that worked before.
- The Divine core of Wanderer armor added its +6 melee also to the arcane strike of Arcana swords and to every hit of summons. Now +6 applies only to the player's own hit.

## Weapons

- Mauls: the 1 s cooldown finally works - before, it did not delay the click, and the maul swung in vain. A click during the cooldown is now fully consumed.
- Toothed sword: damage 30 instead of 35 (it was almost like the halite one, 36). Liopleurodon is between Apalachia (blade 29) and Skythern (31); the bleeding stayed.
- The Tsunami and the Storm Bow in hand in third person are held like a bow, not like a rotated flat item.

## Wanderer armor

- Wraith R8: in a full set the player's body is not drawn, and through the translucent plate armor you see the world, not the skin. The item in hand and the accessories on the body are in place.
- Lunar R8 became dark (blued steel, silver edging, lilac glow). The volumetric crescents above the helmet, on the back and the ridges on the shoulders were removed; the crescent is now a glowing sign on the chest and pauldrons. The piece icons are dark too.
- BiblioCraft armor stand: Wanderer armor on it is no longer overlit - the light is taken from the stand's location (BiblioCraft draws such armor through a fake player with brightness always at maximum).

## Arcana

- Maze: before, any opening on one side was cut straight through, and about 80% of the joints were open - people walked across Arcana in a straight line. Now each chunk on each floor opens one mandatory joint (east or south), Divine's own passages stay with a 30% chance, and the other joints are closed. An opening that runs into a closed joint is walled up with wall masonry - there are no doors in the wall. Joints are rechecked when neighboring chunks are populated: Divine boss halls rebuild the neighboring rooms. Selftest on an 8x8 chunk area: no openings into walls, 59% of joints open, no rooms without an exit on the floor. Applies in new chunks.
- The Memory Crystal no longer writes "crystal issued" every second if the inventory is full in creative mode.

## Other

- Apalachia Cart: a player who gets into it stands still until they press "forward" (before, it started by itself). When the cart moves from the floor to a wall or ceiling, the view turns with it.
- The Cloud Staff does not place clouds in someone else's protected region.
- The staff cloud is called "Skythern Cloud".
- NEI: sorting add-on armor in full sets (helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots) now really works. NEI put it last, after the sort by number, where it no longer decided anything.
- Hotfix: released without a full run of the selftests. The new and affected checks passed; the rendering (mauls, bows, Wraith, Lunar, the stand, the cart) was not viewed in game.
