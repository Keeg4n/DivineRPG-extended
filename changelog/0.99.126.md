## Protected regions and PvP

- The WorldGuard pvp flag on a hybrid server (Crucible, Thermos) is read again: the add-on looked for WorldGuard classes with its own loader and did not find them, so weapon and armor effects in a protected region without PvP hit pets and peaceful mobs. The server log has a line on the first check: "Protected regions: weapon effects are checked against the WorldGuard pvp flag".
- Arcana summons (slime, crab, eye, shark, wraith) hit the player their owner hits, where PvP is allowed. Another player can hurt and kill them; mobs, fire and the owner themselves cannot.
- Harpoons pull a player where PvP is allowed (before, only with melee.pvp), and not in a protected region without PvP. The Divine attractor pulls a player the same way: only where PvP is allowed; the push now reaches the client.
- Lightning of the Storm Sword, Shock Serenade and Storm Bow hits other players again where PvP is allowed; it does not hit the one who summoned it. Mobs and peaceful ones as before.
- The Cloud Staff does not place a cloud inside a creature: a cloud in another player's or a mob's head suffocated it.

## Arcana

- The arcane strike of Arcana swords - no more than once per 0.5 s: before, it stripped the target's invulnerability and hit on every click.
- The arcane strike on a player goes through armor (on mobs - through armor, as before): through armor it hit a player in Terra armor 2-3 times harder than a regular hit. The arcane ward takes its share first.
- Arcana Detonator: every bullet of the volley also hits a player, like blitz pellets (before, one bullet out of 30 reached a player).
- Merik's Firefly and Barrage fly at whatever the player looks at, otherwise at the nearest enemy, and at the middle of the body. Before, at any nearest creature and at the feet, and went into the ground.
- The stars of the star staves slide toward the nearest enemy within 5 blocks of the landing spot.
- LaVekor grenade: besides the explosion - an arcane strike of 12 (grows with the stage) to everyone within 3 blocks.
- Whirlpool, Corrupted Cannon, Ghast Cannon, Sound of Music and Sound of Carols deal arcane damage: the damage is their own, but through armor for mobs, and for a player arcana takes it first. Vethea staves also go through mob armor.
- All-Nighter: damage halved (Divine has 115), arcane, through armor.
- The training dummy shows the copy's arcana and how much the arcane ward took off with the last hit.

## Weapons

- Vethea weapons by kind: claws hit faster than swords (a new hit after 0.35 s), swords as usual, hammers slower (0.7 s) but damage x1.3. The numbers are in the config, the melee.claw and melee.hammer section.
- Vethea discs are thrown like a tomahawk: they are not consumed, hit up to three targets in a row and return to the hand by themselves.
- Ice Serenade immediately freezes hostile mobs near the spot it hit (bosses - a strong slowdown).
- The Shadow Bow fires by itself while the button is held: on every full draw.

## Armor

- Wanderer armor protects more: R6 84%, R7 88%, R8 91% (was 80/83/85). On servers with the old defaults they update automatically.
- Wraith (Flight Crown R8): in flight twice as fast as creative.
- Debug messages of the sets: "Kraken" and "Aquastrive" instead of Kraken and Aquastrive (Russian wording corrected).
