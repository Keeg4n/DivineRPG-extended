## Explorer maps, rails and torches

### Lair maps
- When used, a lair map turns into a regular Minecraft map: centered on the lair, scale 1:4 (512 x 512 blocks), with a red marker on the lair. The terrain is drawn as the player arrives. The chat shows the direction and coordinates, as before.
- The lair map is consumed in the process; if such a map for the same lair already exists, a new one is not issued.
- The tooltip of every map has a line "Summons: ..." - which boss awaits in the lair.

### Apalachia Cart and rails
- The cart shows the name "Apalachia Cart".
- Like the vanilla one: when pushed by a player or mob it rolls on by inertia and slows down, and a moving empty cart seats a mob it hits into itself (not a player, golem or boss).
- Hanging rails on a wall and ceiling keep their turn when a neighbor is broken, and at a fork they switch on a signal, like a switch.

### Bosses and lairs
- Crabs that Carcinus summons in battle drop nothing and give no experience. Burrow crabs from nests drop claws as before.
- Spawners were removed from all add-on structures: tombs, the ice cave, the lair under the jungle temple, Twilight dimension dungeons, crypts and the Mortum tower. They can be turned back on with the `dungeonSpawners` setting. Affects new structures.
- Ayeraco summon messages are received only by players within 64 blocks, not the whole server.

### Other
- In the overworld, within 24 blocks of a Divine torch or a Twilight dimension torch, Divine mobs do not spawn on their own (`divineTorchRadius`). Spawners and summoning are not affected.
- Eden, Wildwood, Apalachia and Skythern Gate Stones are crafted 10 at a time - exactly enough for a portal frame.
