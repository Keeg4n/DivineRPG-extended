## Other mods' tools and protected blocks

- The Blood Magic bound pickaxe, the Thaumcraft drill and other miners no longer break Arcana blocks and ores with mining rules: Twilight dimension ores, twilight stone, jungle ore. Arcana blocks and these ores are also no longer moved by pistons.
- Before, Arcana blocks had normal hardness, and the protection worked only against breaking by a player: anything that digs around it saw plain stone. Now they are unbreakable for everyone, and a player breaks them as before: Arcana blocks with the Arcanite Pickaxe, ore with the pickaxe of its own world.

## Wanderer armor and two-layer skins

- The second skin layer no longer sticks out through Wanderer armor: the armor and the undergarment are raised above it, and the helmets cover the lower edge of the hat at the neck. The silhouette has barely changed, and thin details did not get thicker.
- Open areas stay open: the hands and, for helmets without a visor, the face.

## Selftests

- New test: protected blocks are unbreakable for everyone, a player breaks them with the right tool, a diamond pickaxe does not. On a modpack with Blood Magic, a real bound pickaxe breaks the stone next to them and does not touch protected blocks.
