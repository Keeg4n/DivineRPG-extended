## Blizzard in Iceika: only enclosed places shelter from the wind

The blizzard hits from the side, so a spruce or an awning overhead no longer saves you.

- An enclosed house and the depth of a cave give shelter. If the player can walk through air to the open sky in 12 steps or fewer, the wind reaches them: the cold is three times stronger, it pushes you, and there is a white veil and fog.
- A closed door or trapdoor holds the wind, an open one does not. Glass holds it too, leaves do not.
- A cave entrance is blown through; deeper than 12 steps from the exit it is already quiet.
- Near a fire it warms as before.
