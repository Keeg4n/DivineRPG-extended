## Vethea: foreign items do not work until completion

Until all Vethea bosses are defeated, items not from Vethea stay silent in the dream. Before, the nightmare only inverted the damage of foreign weapons, armor, food and potions, while right-click abilities, accessories and area breaking worked as in the waking world.

- Right-click with a foreign item (Thaumcraft wands and foci, Blood Magic sigils and bound tools, Botania items, summons, teleports, eggs, throwables, a bucket, flint and steel, blocking with a sword) does not trigger. You can: build (blocks, doors, beds, signs, redstone, pots, heads, seeds, paintings), eat and drink (the nightmare inverts it), shoot bows (arrows heal dream mobs). Chests and doors open as usual. Right-click on a creature with an item that has its own action (name tag, lead, shears) does not go through either.
- A hit with a foreign weapon on a dream mob is stopped before the hit: the mob heals by the hit strength, and weapon properties on hit (special Divine effects, lightning, Tinkers effects) do not trigger.
- A foreign tool breaks only the block the player started breaking: Tinkers hammers, excavators and lumber axes, the Terra Shatterer, Treecapitator and other area tools do not work in the dream.
- Foreign Baubles accessories stay silent: on entering Vethea they are taken off (their health, speed and other bonuses go away), and on leaving they are put back on. The items themselves do not go anywhere from the slots.
- Foreign armor does not tick: potions, speed and flight from it (Mutant Creatures, Botania, Thaumcraft armor) do not work in the dream. Divine colored armor (rupee, End) and corrupted items (night vision in darkness, fast digging) stay silent too.
- Weapon lightning (Shock Serenade, Storm Sword, Storm Bow) does not hit dream mobs, and projectile explosions of foreign weapons (Merik Rocket and similar) heal them and do not break blocks.
- Summoning anchors (crab, shark) do not summon in the dream.
- After defeating all Vethea bosses everything works as in the waking world.
- New chat hints on the first attempt: the foreign item does not respond, the tool takes only what is under the hand, jewelry stays silent.

## Blood Magic bound tools and area breaking everywhere

- The Blood Magic bound pickaxe, axe and shovel broke everything around on right-click, bypassing protected regions: in a check in a protected region the pickaxe took down 468 blocks out of 729, the axe 81 logs. Now each block asks the protection, like breaking by hand, and nothing is broken in a protected region. They still do not take the add-on's protected blocks.
- Botania area tools (Terra Shatterer, Terra Axe and others) and Thaumcraft tools and foci (the axe of the stream and others) now break the same way: before, they also broke bypassing protected regions.
