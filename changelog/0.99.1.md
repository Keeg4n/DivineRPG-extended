## Elements of colored Divine gear

The color of Divine and Ender swords and of Ender and Rupee armor now sets an element. Armor can be mixed: each piece of a color gives a quarter of the effect, and a full set of one color gives an extra bonus. Effects are shown in the item tooltip.

### Swords
- Red: sets the target on fire, +20% damage to burning targets.
- Blue: slows the target for 3 s.
- Green: poisons for 4 s, hits undead and spiders 20% harder.
- Yellow: a hit speeds up the wielder for 3 s, blinds a player target.
- Gray: knocks down - strong knockback and a moment of stun (does not move bosses).

### Armor
- Red: fire -25% per piece; a full set sets melee attackers on fire.
- Blue: drowning -25% per piece; a full set - air never runs out.
- Green: poison -25% per piece; a full set removes poison and heals.
- Yellow: +5% speed per piece; a full set - night vision.
- Gray: knockback -25% per piece; a full set - projectile damage -25%.

### Bedrock
- Bedrock Sword: while held, the wielder is not knocked back.
- The Bedrock Maul strikes with a wave: mobs within 3 blocks of the target take 40% of the hit damage and are thrown back.
