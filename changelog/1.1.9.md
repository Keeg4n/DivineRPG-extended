## Fixed

- Mortum, Archer's gallery: rock no longer stands in ribs along the sides of the basilica. Over each stepped buttress the cave was cleared only up to the buttress top, so a column of rock went from it up to the ceiling every 4 blocks. Now the vault above the buttresses is cleared like the rest of the slope.
- Mortum: crypts, gravestones, skulls and giant remains no longer stand in Lethe. They check the riverbed (with a margin of 2 blocks) and do not stand at the water level. Before, a structure could be placed before the river of a neighboring area was poured, and then the river ran right through it.

## Changed

- Mortum arenas: the cave around them is no longer cut like a box. The ceiling above the structure has lumps of up to 5 blocks instead of a flat lid, the vault over the slopes descends unevenly, and the outer border of the cut is ragged instead of a straight line or a perfect circle. Only newly generated arenas change.
