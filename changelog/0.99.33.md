## Dummy, descriptions and fixes from the sheet

### Dummy
- The cape hangs straight and turns together with the dummy.
- After a hit the dummy is invulnerable for half a second, like a player: damage per second matches damage to a live player.
- When you hit the dummy, your pets within 24 blocks go after it (both Divine pets and wraiths from the Wraith Bone).
- No more than 3 dummies on the server across all worlds together.
- The dummy has its own scarecrow texture - minimaps show it instead of the player's head.

### Descriptions and potions
- For all Divine and add-on items without Shift, only the name, enchantments, vanilla attributes and mod name are visible; armor effects, uses, damage and arcana cost went under Shift. The "Shift - description" line is purple.
- With advanced tooltips (F3+H) the name and mod name of arcana potions no longer disappear.
- Arcane potions are named without quotes (Potion Stone Skin) and no longer stack like vanilla ones.

### Other
- The Water Lure Torch burns for up to 8 minutes (still a minute per Arcanium).
- Regular Minecraft sticks can no longer be crafted from the planks of Divine's eight worlds - only sticks of their own world, which work in place of regular ones.
