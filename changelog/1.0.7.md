## Changed

- Self-firing bows (Icicle, Twilight, Wildwood, Skythern, Halite and Shadow bows): while Shift is held they work like ordinary bows - draw and release by hand. Let go of Shift and they fire by themselves again.
- Arrows nobody can pick up - shot with Infinity, from the Endless Quiver, from bows that need no arrows, extra arrows of multi-shot bows - vanish 5 seconds after sticking in a block instead of lying there for a minute. Vanilla and Divine arrows alike. Arrows that can be picked up and arrows of skeletons and other mobs stay as before.

## Other

- Selftests for bows, quivers, pellets and arrow cleanup were run.
