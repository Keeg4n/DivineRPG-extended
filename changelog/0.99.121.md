## Summons really eat arcana

- Summons did not trigger the arcana regeneration pause. Arcana regenerates 20 per second, so any summons lasted forever from the first minute, without a single upgrade.
- Now all summons (slime, crab, eye, shark, wraith) cost the same - 10 arcana per second each. They pay once per second with a single regular arcana expense for all at once, and this expense sets the regeneration pause: while summons are alive, arcana does not regenerate by itself.
- The payment follows the regular arcana rules: the discount of the full Korma set (-20%) and Composure, a shorter pause with the Crystal of Clarity, no pause under the arcana potion (Clarity), a bigger supply with Korma and Vatticus quests, regeneration twice as fast with the arcana Crown R8.
- How long summons live (supply 200 unless stated otherwise):
  - without upgrades: 2 summons - about 11 s, 1 summon - about 21 s;
  - Crystal of Clarity: 2 - about 17 s;
  - full Korma set: 2 - about 16 s, 3 - about 11 s; with the Crystal of Clarity: 2 - about 31 s, 3 - about 16 s;
  - arcana Crown R8 with the Crystal of Clarity: 2 and 3 summons last indefinitely;
  - under the arcana potion - indefinitely, as long as the potion lasts.
- Hotfix: released without a full run of the selftests. The summon checks passed (two crabs on 200 arcana without upgrades live 12 s, with Clarity - the whole check); not checked in game.
