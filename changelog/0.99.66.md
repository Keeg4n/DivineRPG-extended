## Mob spawning by rank

- Mobs above the rank are now cut off when the mob kind is chosen, not when it spawns. Before, the game picked a pack kind from the whole biome list and only then refused a mob above the rank - the whole pack attempt was lost, and for low-rank players noticeably fewer mobs spawned (in measurement - 67-88% of normal). Now the kind is chosen only from those allowed by the ranks of nearby players, and spawn density for weak players does not sag (in measurement - not lower than without ranks). The rules are the same: in the overworld and the Nether the weakest player in the spawn zone (8 chunks) decides, in the End - the strongest. The check at spawn time remains as a safeguard for spawners, summoning and spawning by other mods.
- Creatures that the game settles during generation of new chunks also go through rank selection. There was no check there at all - so while exploring the world a rank 0 player could get Glacons (Divine also registers them as creatures).
- The spawn cycle became faster (1.4-2.3 times in measurements): the game no longer spends attempts on mobs it will reject anyway.

## Selftest

- A new test: seven land sites with rank mobs in the biome are chosen across a 10000 x 10000 square, with rank 0-3 players and a 0+3 pair nearby; at night 20 cycles of real spawning are run without ranks, the old way and the new way. It checks: no forbidden mobs, density for weak players not below 80% of spawning without ranks, the cycle not slower than the old one.
