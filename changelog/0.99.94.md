## Blizzard Spruce

During an Iceika blizzard a mini-boss with its own model arrives: a walking spruce in snow on two roots.

- Once per blizzard, in the first tens of seconds, to the player the wind reaches, 20-32 blocks from them. When the blizzard ends, it leaves on its own, without loot.
- 300 health, no knockback. Throws three snow clumps in a fan, each one hurts and adds cold. Calls snowmen, up to three: they also throw clumps, and each clump adds +8 cold.
- Nether weapons hit both it and the snowmen twice as hard and set them on fire, like all cold mobs; fire armor halves their damage.
- Loot: 6-10 snowflakes, 4-8 ice shards, 1-2 ice stones.

For testing: /dreblizzard start, then /dreminiboss.
