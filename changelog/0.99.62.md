## Easter egg

- Musou no Hitotachi: the curl on the guard was redone. Before it was a tight spiral of thick tube - the coils stuck together into a solid purple disc, and from the side it looked like a ball above the guard. Now, as on the in-game model, a thin glowing line comes out of the guard, goes around the edge at the base of the blade and curls inward in an open spiral (the coils do not stick together); on the spine side at the base is a short glowing tongue. Both are flat, like inlay, and visible from both sides of the sword.
