## Liopleurodon - a late fish boss

- Liopleurodon became an optional boss between Apalachia and Skythern. By itself it no longer appears.
- Bloody Bait: a shapeless craft from a Shark Pup (fish), raw beef and an Apalachia piece. Keep it in the inventory and fish in the open ocean of the Overworld - instead of a catch, Liopleurodon surfaces. The bait is consumed. While it is alive, a second one is not summoned. It is not summoned near the shore.
- The fight is only in the ocean: you can hurt it from the water, from a boat or swimming over the ocean. From the shore and from a pillar in the sea, hits and shots do not go through. If it is lured out of the ocean, it gets angry: damage x1.5.
- Moves: a ram with knockback (knocks you off a boat). Below 60% - a whirlpool that pulls toward the maw and down, and shark pups. Below 30% - an arcing leap with an area hit and water jets at those not in water. 600 health (setting liopleurodonHealth).
- Loot from the body: a skull, 4-8 liopleurodon teeth, an ocean crate.
- Personal loot for each attacker: the Tsunami bow (guaranteed for the first victory, then 30%), the Liopleurodon Anchor (30%), Liopleurodon Fins (accessory).
- Tsunami: a bow that fires a fan of 5 arrows for one regular arrow (each 10-20 damage). Only the middle one can be picked up. Does not break.
- Liopleurodon Fins: a fourth accessory, for the legs. In water you swim faster, and the sprint key in water is a dash forward once every 2 seconds. On the body they are visible under the sole on all armor profiles.

## Toothy weapons and mauls

- Toothed sword: damage 35 (Skythern level) and bleeding. A hit mob loses 4 health per second through armor for 5 seconds. Does not affect players.
- Toothed maul: damage 25 and an area hit, like the Bedrock one. Mobs of the same kind within 3.5 blocks of the target take 25 each. Does not affect players and pets.
- All mauls (water, bedrock, crab, fury, corrupted, Terran, toothed): a real cooldown of 1 second. You cannot even swing ahead of time, and a hit "without damage" no longer exists.

## Summons: crab - shark - wraith

- The crab anchor (from Carcinus: guaranteed for the first victory, then 25%) summons a crab. The crab runs on the ground and hits the owner's target with its claws (4 arcane damage x Arcana stage). Costs 2 arcana per second, no more than 2 at once.
- The Liopleurodon Anchor (from Liopleurodon, 30%) summons a shark. The shark flies nearby and bites the owner's target (8 x stage). Costs 3 arcana per second, no more than 2.
- Both anchors: right-click only summons, there is no throw. Not craftable. Drawn as real anchors: the crab one red-orange, the Liopleurodon one dark blue.
- Wraith bone: Captain Merik sells it for 12 arcanium only to those who defeated the Wind Lord (Skythern). It was removed from Arcana ancient chests.
- The shark and whale anchors were renamed to harpoons (Shark Harpoon, Bowhead Harpoon). The texture is the same, crafted as before.
