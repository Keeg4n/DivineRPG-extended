## Hanging lakes of Vethea and Divine boss minions

- On Vethea floors, stalactites with Dream Water lakes hang from the ceiling (`vetheaHangingLakeRarity`). Dream Water flows upward and is infinite like regular water; in it, up and down are swapped, and you can fish in it. Affects new chunks.
- Dream Water Bucket.
- Divine bosses' own summons (King of Scorchers, Karot, Eternal Archer, Reyvor, the Watcher, Quadro and others) are now minions too: no loot or experience, and they die together with the boss. Spawner mobs near arenas and the boss's next form are not affected.
