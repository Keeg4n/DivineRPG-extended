## Changed
- Mini-bosses come only at their moment, not just once per pause:
  - Eden, Noon Cori - you are overheated to half of the scale or more and still heating up (under open sky, not in water); in the shade or in water it does not come;
  - Wildwood, Old Verek - only in a downpour;
  - Apalachia, Stonebreaker - only in a rockfall;
  - Skythern, Sky Grabber - you are at an island edge or over the abyss (a drop of 24 blocks within 4 blocks of you);
  - Mortum, Mourner - you have killed 6 mobs in a row (no more than a minute between kills);
  - Vethea floor 3, Sleepwalker - 8 dream mobs in a row;
  - Iceika, Blizzard Spruce - in a blizzard, as before.
- The pause between mini-bosses (miniBossMinutes) still applies on top of it. New config option miniBossConditions (true by default; false - by the pause only). A world weather turned off in the config does not block its mini-boss.
## Other
- Selftest for mini-boss moments: overheat under the sky and under a roof, downpour and rockfall on and off, over the abyss and on a wide floor, kill streaks and their break.
