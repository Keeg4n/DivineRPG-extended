## Changed
- Twilight Demon - Mage spire (new chunks only: a tower that is already built stays as it is): six gothic fins rise from the platform past the wall top into pinnacles with lamps (the south one starts over the door), tall lamp windows between them and purple brick belts on every floor, a crown terrace with a lantern tier and a needle of purple brick with obsidian rows and a lamp on the tip; over the crown a halo of floating stones with lamps, around the tower three floating crystals. The balcony, the turrets and the skull cages under them are gone. The fight is unchanged: the floors, the staircase, both doors, the crown hall with the focus on its pedestal.
## Fixed
- The netherite brick of the 1.1.3 arena looks (the basilica roof and its towers, the edges of the black crystal, the rim of the moon) was laid as plain nether brick: the Divine colored bricks are one block with the color in its metadata. New chunks get the real netherite brick.
- Divine mob and boss sounds did not follow the creature volume sliders: Divine's sound list puts them into "master", so only the master volume reached them. Now hostile mobs and bosses are under "Hostile Creatures", the friendly ones (the bunny, Jack-o-Man and the Livestock Merchant, the Grizzle, Wraith and Golem of Rejuvenation pets) under "Friendly Creatures". Weapon and item sounds stay where Divine put them, under "Friendly Creatures", as vanilla does with the bow. A resource pack that sets its own category for a Divine sound keeps it.
